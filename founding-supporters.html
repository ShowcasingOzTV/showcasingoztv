FOUNDING SUPPORTERS

Help Put ShowcasingOz TV
On The Road

ShowcasingOz TV has been built around a simple idea: travel Australia, capture the places and people that make it extraordinary, and give destinations, attractions, accommodation providers and small businesses a platform to tell their stories.

In 2027, we plan to take that idea nationwide. But a production of this scale cannot happen on ambition alone. That is where our Founding Supporters come in.

Be Part of the Story From the Beginning

By supporting ShowcasingOz TV at its beginning, you are not simply contributing to another crowdfunding campaign.

You are helping put our production crew on the road, helping us reach communities across Australia, and helping create the first two seasons of a new Australian travel and tourism media platform.

If we succeed, you will always be able to say: “I helped make ShowcasingOz TV happen from the very beginning.”

What Your Support Helps Make Possible

Producing a national travel series means far more than turning on a camera. Your support contributes toward the real costs required to take a professional production crew across Australia.

🎥

Production

Professional filming equipment, production resources, editing and the people required to bring each destination to life.

🚐

Life on the Road

Transport, accommodation, travel logistics and the day-to-day costs of moving a production crew across the country.

🌏

Australian Communities

Reaching towns, attractions, businesses and communities that deserve the opportunity to be seen by a wider audience.

💻

Digital Distribution

Editing, publishing and distributing destination content across the ShowcasingOz TV website and digital platforms.

🏞️

Experiences

Capturing attractions, landmarks, experiences and hidden gems that help tell a richer story about the places we visit.

📣

Building the Audience

Helping grow awareness of ShowcasingOz TV and the Australian destinations, operators and stories featured along the journey.

A Supporter, Not a Sponsor

Founding Supporters are individuals who want to help ShowcasingOz TV get on the road and become part of the project's beginning.

These supporter rewards are separate from our commercial sponsorship, destination partnership and business filming opportunities, which are designed for organisations seeking promotional exposure and commercial integration.
