Support ShowcasingOz TV

Help us showcase Australian destinations and bring greater exposure to the accommodation providers, attractions, small businesses and communities behind them.

ShowcasingOz TV is building a national travel media platform designed to capture and share the places, people and experiences that make Australia worth exploring.

Our planned journey includes major cities, regional towns, coastal communities, outback destinations, tourism operators, local businesses, landmarks and hidden gems.

Filming for Season 1 is planned to commence on 27 January 2027. You can explore the destinations and filming route on our Filming Map.

Behind every locally owned accommodation property, attraction, tour, café and small business are real people working to build a livelihood and contribute to their local community. Many of them need more than encouragement. They need the opportunity to be seen.

Why this project matters

When travellers choose to stop, stay and explore, their spending reaches far beyond the destination itself. It can support accommodation providers, attractions, hospitality businesses, retailers, tour operators and the people they employ.

However, many worthwhile destinations and locally owned businesses struggle to receive the same exposure as Australia’s best-known tourism locations.

ShowcasingOz TV has been created to help bring those destinations, businesses and stories to a wider audience through professional filming, photography and travel storytelling.

What your support will help make possible

  • Put a professional three-person production crew on the road
  • Film Australian cities, towns, attractions, accommodation and local businesses
  • Capture landmarks, experiences, community stories and hidden gems
  • Produce professional travel content for our website, YouTube and social platforms
  • Encourage audiences to discover and explore more of Australia
  • Give participating destinations and tourism businesses greater visibility

Why support is needed now

The filming route and production schedule have been extensively planned, but a national production cannot commence without the upfront funding required for crew, equipment, transport, accommodation, insurance, post-production and other essential operating costs.

ShowcasingOz TV previously rescheduled its filming commencement because sufficient funding had not been secured. We are not prepared to repeatedly announce new dates without the financial foundation required to deliver the production responsibly.

If sufficient funding cannot be secured for the 2027 production, ShowcasingOz TV’s planned national filming rollout will not proceed.

Our Pozible Campaign Launches 15 September

We are launching an all-or-nothing Pozible campaign seeking the public support required to bring ShowcasingOz TV to life.

Because the campaign is all-or-nothing, the full target must be reached for ShowcasingOz TV to receive the pledged funds.

Every pledge made through Pozible will count towards the campaign target. If you cannot pledge, sharing the campaign can still help it reach someone who can.

Become a Founding Supporter

Those who support the Pozible campaign will have the opportunity to become ShowcasingOz TV Founding Supporters: the people who helped move the project from planning into production.

Depending on the reward selected, Founding Supporter benefits may include website recognition, episode credits, supporter merchandise, travel benefits and ShowcasingOz TV Picture Books.

Support is welcomed at every level. This campaign is not dependent on one person funding an entire production. It depends on enough people deciding that this is a project they want to help make possible.

This is not simply about creating another travel series. It is about creating an opportunity to showcase Australian destinations and the people, businesses and communities that make them worth visiting.

Community support contributes to the overall ShowcasingOz TV production and does not purchase editorial control or guarantee the inclusion of a particular business. If you represent an accommodation property, attraction, business, council or tourism organisation and would like to discuss a commercial partnership, please contact us.
