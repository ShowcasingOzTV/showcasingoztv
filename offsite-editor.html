🎞️ Turn the Footage Into Something People Want to Watch

Our videographers capture the destinations. Our presenter introduces the places and experiences. Your job is to bring that work together into an engaging, exciting and professionally finished production.

The Offsite Video Editor will undertake the majority of ShowcasingOz TV's editing work. Your decisions about pacing, shot selection, sound, colour and presentation will shape how viewers experience the series and how the ShowcasingOz TV brand is represented.

This is a central production role with a direct influence on the quality of everything we release.

We need an experienced editor who can recognise strong material, make purposeful creative decisions and deliver work that the crew, featured businesses and viewers can be proud of.

Help Showcase Australia Through Your Editing

ShowcasingOz TV is being developed to highlight cities, towns, regional communities, accommodation, attractions, landmarks and hidden gems across Australia. Content will be distributed through our website, YouTube and social platforms.

Your work could move from a coastal destination to a country motel, wildlife attraction, historic landmark, local business or outback experience. The variety calls for an editor who can make each destination appealing while maintaining a consistent production style across the series.

Our aim is to give people reasons to explore Australia, visit attractions, book accommodation and support local businesses. The finished edit needs to make those places easy to understand and enjoyable to discover.

🎥 The Role You Will Play

You will take the lead on assembling and finishing the majority of our video content, working with material supplied by two travelling videographers and the presenter. The videographers will contribute to editing and media preparation where required, but this role provides the main offsite post-production capacity.

  • Review, organise and select footage from both videographers.
  • Build rough cuts and develop them into polished destination and travel content.
  • Integrate presenting, interviews, location footage and supporting shots into clear, engaging sequences.
  • Produce shorter edits, trailers, previews and vertical social content from the available material.
  • Balance dialogue, music, ambience and sound effects for clear, consistent audio.
  • Correct exposure and colour, and match footage between cameras and locations.
  • Add appropriate titles, location names, captions, graphics and agreed branding.
  • Prepare review versions, implement consolidated feedback and deliver final exports.
  • Maintain organised project files, media references, versions and backups.
  • Check the finished video before delivery rather than relying on others to find avoidable errors.

⭐ The Finished Product Represents Our Brand

Strong footage can lose its impact through poor editing. Equally, a considered edit can bring the best out of the material and give viewers a compelling reason to keep watching.

We are looking for professional judgement, not simply someone who can join clips together or apply effects. Music, transitions and graphics should support the destination and the viewing experience. The edit should feel confident, coherent and purposeful.

  • Choose strong opening sequences and maintain suitable pacing throughout.
  • Keep dialogue intelligible and avoid distracting changes in audio level.
  • Maintain consistent colour, titles, fonts and presentation across releases.
  • Check spelling, location names, captions and any featured business details.
  • Use footage in its correct context and flag missing information rather than guessing.
  • Use approved music, graphics and other assets with appropriate usage rights.
  • Avoid abrupt cuts, missing media, black frames, unfinished graphics and export errors.

🤝 Close Collaboration Is Essential

You will work closely with both videographers and the business founder. This requires clear communication about the footage received, the intended edit, production priorities and the standard expected before release.

You must be comfortable receiving direction, contributing informed ideas and acting on feedback professionally.

We want someone who asks useful questions early, identifies problems promptly and keeps the team informed.

  • Strong spoken and written English for production discussions and feedback.
  • Confirm the brief, priorities and delivery requirements before beginning an edit.
  • Provide progress updates and realistic estimates of completion times.
  • Flag missing shots, audio problems, file-transfer issues or unclear instructions promptly.
  • Give constructive feedback to the videographers where it can improve subsequent filming.
  • Use time-coded notes and organised review versions to make revisions efficient.
  • Coordinate with the founder on final approval before content is released.

🧰 Required Experience and Technical Skills

This role requires substantial professional video-editing experience, supported by a strong portfolio. Practical videography experience and a sound understanding of camera coverage, composition, lighting and audio capture are also important.

  • Advanced proficiency in Adobe Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve.
  • Experience completing substantial edits from raw footage through to final delivery.
  • Strong judgement in shot selection, sequence construction, pacing and continuity.
  • Experience handling footage from multiple cameras, codecs and recording formats.
  • Confident colour correction, camera matching, dialogue editing and audio mixing.
  • Ability to prepare professional horizontal and vertical versions for different platforms.
  • Experience with titles, captions and clean, restrained motion graphics.
  • Reliable media organisation, proxy workflows, project handovers and backups.
  • Ability to work efficiently with large volumes of footage and changing priorities.
  • Consistent attention to detail and a reliable record of meeting agreed deadlines.

Travel, tourism, lifestyle, documentary, interview or branded-content experience would be particularly relevant. Show us completed work that demonstrates your editing decisions, not only a montage of attractive shots.

💻 Remote Setup and File Handling

You will need access to a capable editing workstation, suitable storage, reliable internet and appropriately licensed editing software. The workflow will involve receiving large media files and returning review versions, finished exports and project files.

Tell us about your computer, software, internet connection and experience with remote collaboration. Software access, storage, transfer methods, backup responsibilities and any additional requirements will be agreed before engagement. Please do not purchase equipment or subscriptions in anticipation of an offer.

Unreleased footage, business information and production material must be handled securely. Publication, portfolio use and access permissions will be agreed with the founder.

🕒 Hours, Communication and Delivery Expectations

We will agree working arrangements and reasonable communication overlap with the Australian production team before commencement. You must be reachable during agreed times and communicate early if a deadline is at risk.

Episode lengths, output volume, release priorities and review stages will be agreed against the available editing time and complexity of the material. We are seeking efficient, dependable delivery, with quality maintained throughout.
