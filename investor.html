Strategic Partnership & Investment Opportunities

ShowcasingOz TV is preparing to launch Season 1 of a national Australian tourism, travel, and destination production commencing 27 July 2026. This is not a single campaign or short-term content project, it is being structured as a scalable, multi-season commercial platform designed to showcase towns, tourism regions, accommodation providers, attractions, selected businesses, and regional Australia through destination-led storytelling, strategic partnerships, and long-term digital asset creation.

We are currently finalising aligned strategic partners and investors to help activate the first national rollout and establish the commercial, operational, and strategic foundation required to launch ShowcasingOz TV at full strength.

Why This Opportunity Exists

Across Australia, countless towns, tourism operators, accommodation providers, attractions, and regional economies remain significantly underexposed despite strong visitor appeal and economic relevance.

ShowcasingOz TV has been created to help bridge that visibility gap by combining travel storytelling, tourism promotion, commercial integration, sponsorship, and destination discovery into a structured platform capable of generating both regional exposure and commercial return.

The objective is not simply to film Australia, it is to build a commercially structured tourism media and discovery ecosystem.

Season 1 Rollout

Season 1 is structured as an approximately 20-week national filming rollout spanning more than 100 towns and locations, with approximately 40+ primary overnight destinations forming the operational backbone of the first production cycle.

  • National route commencing 27 July 2026
  • Approx. 20-week structured production rollout
  • 100+ towns and locations
  • Regional tourism, accommodation, attractions, councils, and small business commercial pathways
  • Foundation for future seasons, route expansion, and scalable tourism corridor growth

Two Strategic Pathways

To support launch, ShowcasingOz TV is currently open to two primary forms of aligned backing depending on investor type, strategic relevance, and preferred involvement.

Option 1 — Founding Strategic Partner (Corporate / Business / Brand)

Designed for a business, tourism group, accommodation network, corporate brand, or strategic organisation wanting more than passive investment. This pathway is structured around major strategic integration, high-visibility commercial rights, and top-tier platform alignment.

  • $175,000 strategic capital commitment target
  • Founding Strategic Partner / Major Sponsor rights
  • Highest sponsor hierarchy across ShowcasingOz TV
  • Premium website, apparel, episode, and promotional visibility
  • Long-term strategic commercial positioning (2026–2028)
  • Potential category protections and first-mover strategic advantages
  • Priority integration across broader commercial pathways where aligned

Option 2 — Private Investment Opportunity (Individual / Angel / Silent Capital)

Designed for private investors or aligned individuals seeking equity participation in the launch and commercial growth of ShowcasingOz TV without requiring public-facing sponsorship integration.

  • Target raise: Up to $175,000
  • Equity participation opportunity (structure subject to investment scale and strategic fit)
  • Early-stage entry into a scalable tourism and media platform
  • Commercial upside through platform growth, sponsorship expansion, and multi-channel revenue model
  • Suitable for aligned investors seeking capital participation over sponsor visibility

Detailed investment structure, equity terms, and supporting documentation are available during direct discussions with serious aligned parties.
