Investment Opportunity

Help fund the launch of a new and exciting Australian tourism media business.

ShowcasingOz TV is seeking capital to turn a planned national travel production into a sustainable operating business. Our preferred approach is equity investment from an investor, or a group of investors, who see commercial potential in a founder-led Australian tourism media platform.

Your capital would not only help us secure the equipment, crew, transport and working funds needed to begin production, but also help us grow our brand throughout Seasons 1 and 2 to acquire income and build profit. Revenue will be generated by showcasing destinations, accommodation, attractions and small businesses across Australia, with a particular focus on regional tourism.

The Business You Would Be Backing

Australia offers far more than its best-known visitor destinations. Cities, towns, rural areas and remote communities have accommodation, attractions, landmarks, hidden gems, and experiences that give travellers reasons to visit, stay longer and spend locally.

ShowcasingOz TV plans to bring these places together through professional travel and lifestyle video, destination information and accommodation booking links. Content will be distributed through our website, YouTube and social platforms.

For visitors, the aim is practical inspiration: where to go, where to stay, what to see, and what to do. For tourism operators, the aim is greater exposure to potential customers. For the business, the opportunity is to earn revenue through production services, sponsorship and travel referrals while developing an audience and a catalogue of destination content.

Where the Business Stands Today

ShowcasingOz TV is at the pre-production stage. A public website and destination pages are in place, accommodation booking links operate through our Booking.com affiliation, and route planning, operator outreach and crew expressions of interest have been undertaken.

The national filming rollout has been postponed because sufficient funding has not yet been secured. Production dates will be confirmed once the required capital and operational arrangements are in place.

We are also planning a pilot focused on showcasing one designated Australian town to demonstrate the proposed production format. Its location and filming arrangements will be announced when confirmed. The pilot is a separate step from funding the national rollout, however, we would also accept offers to fund this introductory production.

How the Business Plans to Earn Revenue

The proposed model combines services sold to tourism businesses and commercial partners with income from eligible travel bookings. Each revenue source depends on attracting paying customers, delivering the agreed work and building relevant visitor interest.

  • Filming packages: paid production and promotional packages for accommodation providers, attractions and small businesses.
  • Sponsorship: agreed brand placements and promotional benefits for businesses supporting the production.
  • Destination promotion: commissioned work with councils, tourism organisations and other destination partners.
  • Accommodation booking commissions: income from eligible bookings made through our Booking.com affiliate links.

These are planned revenue sources, not a statement of secured contracts or guaranteed income. Any investment assessment needs to examine customer demand, pricing, sales conversion, production costs and the time required to develop the audience.

What Your Capital Would Enable

The funding requirement is about more than purchasing cameras. A viable launch needs sufficient working capital to produce, edit and distribute content while sales and booking revenue develop.

  • Professional camera, sound, lighting, storage and production equipment.
  • Crew recruitment, onboarding and production payments.
  • Vehicle arrangements, fuel, travel and crew accommodation.
  • Insurance, communications, software and operating expenses.
  • Editing, post-production and digital distribution.
  • Sales activity, destination coordination and launch preparation.
  • Working capital and a contingency allowance for production disruption.

We are seeking $250,000 in capital to fund the national launch of ShowcasingOz TV. This funding would support production equipment, crew recruitment, transport, accommodation, post-production and the working capital required to commence and sustain the initial production. Investment structure and equity participation would be discussed directly with prospective investors.

A Smaller First Step: Fund the Pilot

We are also seeking $4,000 to fund a pilot production focused on one Australian town. The pilot would demonstrate how ShowcasingOz TV will showcase a destination, its accommodation, attractions and local businesses, giving prospective investors and viewers a practical example of the proposed production.

If you would prefer to support this initial production before considering a larger commitment, we welcome a discussion. The form of backing, use of funds and any agreed benefits would be confirmed in writing. Funding the pilot alone would not fund the national rollout or automatically provide an ownership interest in the business.

Preferred Approach: Investment in a Founder-Led Business

The preferred route is capital investment with an agreed ownership interest, rather than an appointment to run the production. Our business founder intends to continue leading the direction and day-to-day development of ShowcasingOz TV.

The capital contribution, valuation, ownership percentage, reporting arrangements, decision-making rights and any investor involvement would need to be negotiated and documented. No fixed equity percentage or investment return is advertised here. Any equity arrangement would also depend on establishing or confirming the appropriate business structure and meeting applicable requirements.

Alternative: A Partnership That Includes Capital

A business partnership may also be considered where capital contribution forms part of the arrangement. Relevant expertise or industry connections could complement that funding, but would not replace the need for capital.

Any such arrangement would require clear agreement on funding, responsibilities, ownership and decision-making, with a shared understanding of the production's direction.

Building Beyond the First Season

Your investment would help establish the production capability and commercial foundations for a business designed to operate across multiple destinations and seasons.

Professional equipment, production processes, tourism operator relationships and a growing catalogue of destination content would support future filming and commercial activity. Each production would also provide an opportunity to demonstrate our work to prospective customers, sponsors and destination partners.

The planned revenue model combines paid filming packages, sponsorship, destination promotion and eligible accommodation booking commissions. This provides several commercial avenues to develop, rather than relying solely on audience growth or advertising income.

Our approach is to match production commitments to available funding, monitor costs and sales, and use the results of the initial production to guide further expansion. The business plan, financial assumptions and key risks would form part of direct investment discussions.

This is an early-stage business opportunity. Capital is at risk, and financial returns are not guaranteed.

What We Need to Establish Together

An initial discussion will establish whether your investment objectives and our funding needs are aligned. Before any investment proceeds, the review should cover:

  • The business structure, ownership and proposed investment terms.
  • The production scope, launch budget and use of capital.
  • Cash-flow assumptions, revenue targets and a slower-sales scenario.
  • The status of operator interest, sponsorship discussions and any contracts.
  • Production milestones, reporting and the conditions required to commence.
  • Key risks, investor involvement and the basis for any potential return.

These matters will be addressed through direct discussions and supporting documents, with plans and assumptions distinguished from confirmed arrangements.
