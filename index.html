PRODUCTION UPDATE

A Note From The Founder - Funding For Filming

Four years ago, I came up with what I believed, and still firmly believe, is a wonderful concept. I wanted to design a business that could capture all the amazing landmarks, attractions, hidden gems, along with the captivating cities, towns, and regions that span the length and breadth of our beautiful country, Australia, and then showcase them to the world.

In the process, I also have a passion for helping deliver greater exposure to small businesses, accommodations, and attractions, especially in regional areas, to help them gain an uplift in visitor numbers, to help grow their business, and to ultimately contribute to growing local economies in the process. I cannot express in words the countless hours, the research, the effort, and the funding I have already contributed to making this vision a reality, and sadly, without any success to date.

I genuinely believed that there would be numerous companies, businesses, tourism organisations, investors, and individuals who would jump on this wonderful opportunity, and partner with us to showcase our beautiful country, to help boost tourism, and to support our cause in driving visitor numbers, especially in regional areas. I also genuinely believed that numerous companies, businesses, tourism organisations, investors, and individuals would help contribute financially to what we are trying to build. Unfortunately, I was mistaken.

Whilst the support has been amazing on social media and from local businesses in areas we were scheduled to visit in Season 1, there has been minimal support from a financial perspective. What we have discovered is that very few investors have an appetite to fund such a business as ours, and much of the support from the community has come in the form of cheerleading rather than contribution. The decision for many people not to contribute financially is completely understandable, considering the ongoing pressures around the high cost-of-living. Unfortunately, it does leave us in a position where filming and production becomes impossible, at this point in time. Therefore, it is with great regret that we announce a secondary postponement to our national filming rollout scheduled to commence in January 2027.

We completely understand the disappointment this causes the businesses, communities and supporters waiting for production to begin, and we absolutely share that same disappointment. I'm using this opportunity to provide a clear explanation behind our decision.

It's no secret that professional production involves substantial costs, especially with a tourism and travel media business such as ours. These costs include crew wages, professional cameras and sound equipment, transport, fuel, crew accommodation, insurance and editing. This means that a certain level of funding needs to be obtained prior to the commencement of filming and production in order to, not only cover these costs, but to also ensure the business is sustainable. We want to place ourselves in a position that we can continue showcasing all regions around the country beyond where we have scheduled to film in Seasons 1 and 2.

The fact is, we have still been unable to secure the funding required to proceed with the production responsibly. We cannot, and will not, commit to filming dates without the required resources to deliver what we promise, nor are we prepared to begin a journey we cannot afford to sustain.

Public support and commercial backing are essential to getting ShowcasingOz TV started. We will continue to pursue investors, sponsors and other funding sources alongside supporter pledges. We will announce confirmed filming dates when adequate funding and production arrangements are in place.

The financial target we have set to comfortably proceed with production is $250,000. Please do not feel that even a small contribution wouldn't make any difference, because it absolutely would. If just 270,000 people each contributed $1 towards our business, our vision would progress to production, and we could commence with filming and supporting the numerous small businesses, attractions, accommodations, towns, and regions as we desired from the outset. You have an opportunity to make a pledge by clicking the button below. I sincerely hope you are as passionate as I am about travel and tourism, but especially around helping out small businesses, attractions, and accommodations, so we can work towards growing local economies and tourism. Any contribution you make would be greatly appreciated.

Showcasing Australia, One Destination at a Time

ShowcasingOz TV is an Australian travel and lifestyle media platform created to showcase the destinations, people, businesses and experiences that make this country worth exploring.

Our planned filming journey includes major cities, regional towns, coastal communities and outback destinations. We will capture accommodation, attractions, small businesses, landmarks, experiences, hidden gems and the people behind the places we visit.

Content will be shared through the ShowcasingOz TV website, YouTube and social platforms, helping audiences discover more of Australia and the local tourism businesses that bring its destinations to life.

ShowcasingOz TV is being developed as a commercially structured tourism media platform, with planned revenue generated through:

  • Filming packages with accommodation providers, attractions and small businesses
  • Sponsorship placements across episodes and digital distribution
  • Destination partnerships with councils, tourism organisations and regional bodies
  • Travel-related affiliate and booking revenue

This model is intended to support the long-term growth of the platform while creating valuable exposure opportunities for Australian tourism operators.

About ShowcasingOz TV

We are building an independent Australian travel platform to showcase the places and experiences that give people a reason to visit, stay longer and support local tourism businesses, from major cities to rural and remote communities.

🎥 What We Plan to Do

Produce professional travel and lifestyle content showcasing Australian destinations, accommodation, attractions, small businesses, landmarks, experiences and hidden gems.

🌏 Who We Plan to Work With

Work with accommodation providers, attraction owners, local businesses, councils, tourism organisations and other partners along our national filming route.

🚐 Why It Matters

Tourism supports real people and local livelihoods. Greater visibility can help audiences discover places and businesses they may never otherwise have known existed.
