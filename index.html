Australia’s Regional Travel & Tourism Platform

ShowcasingOz TV is a national travel and lifestyle platform dedicated to promoting regional Australia through professionally produced video content.

We work with accommodation providers, attractions, small businesses, and tourism organisations to capture and showcase destinations, experiences, and stories to both domestic and international audiences.

ShowcasingOz TV operates as a commercially structured tourism media platform, with revenue generated through:

  • Partnered filming packages with accommodation providers, attractions, and small businesses along the filming route
  • Integrated sponsorship placements across episodes and digital distribution
  • Destination promotion partnerships with tourism organisations and regional bodies

This model enables scalable revenue generation across multiple regions while supporting local tourism operators.

About ShowcasingOz TV

ShowcasingOz TV is Australia’s leading independent travel and tourism platform — dedicated to promoting regional destinations, local attractions, and the people behind them.

🎥 What We Do

We produce engaging travel and lifestyle video content that highlights the best of Australia’s regions — from coastal escapes to outback adventures — shared through our digital and television channels.

🌏 Who We Work With

We partner with tourism boards, councils, and small businesses to tell their stories, boost local tourism, and showcase their unique value to a national and global audience.

🚐 Why It Matters

We believe every Australian town has a story worth telling. Our mission is to help regional communities thrive by connecting travelers with authentic, homegrown experiences.
