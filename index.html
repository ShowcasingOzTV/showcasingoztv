Showcasing Australia, One Destination at a Time

ShowcasingOz TV is an Australian travel and lifestyle media platform created to showcase the destinations, people, businesses and experiences that make this country worth exploring.

Our planned filming journey includes major cities, regional towns, coastal communities and outback destinations. We will capture accommodation, attractions, small businesses, landmarks, experiences, hidden gems and the people behind the places we visit.

Content will be shared through the ShowcasingOz TV website, YouTube and social platforms, helping audiences discover more of Australia and the local tourism businesses that bring its destinations to life.

ShowcasingOz TV is being developed as a commercially structured tourism media platform, with planned revenue generated through:

  • Filming packages with accommodation providers, attractions and small businesses
  • Sponsorship placements across episodes and digital distribution
  • Destination partnerships with councils, tourism organisations and regional bodies
  • Travel-related affiliate and booking revenue

This model is intended to support the long-term growth of the platform while creating valuable exposure opportunities for Australian tourism operators.
