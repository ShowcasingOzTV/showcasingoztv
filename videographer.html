🇦🇺 Imagine Your Career Taking You Around Australia

Imagine having a career opportunity that doesn't keep you behind the same desk, in the same studio, or shooting the same kinds of projects week after week.

Instead, your workplace changes constantly.

One week you could be capturing spectacular Australian coastline. The next, you could be filming an historic site, wildlife park, museum, art gallery, winery, adventure attraction, iconic landmark or one of the country's lesser-known hidden gems.

You'll travel through cities, country towns, regional communities and remote destinations, experiencing the places, people and stories that make Australia extraordinary while capturing it all on film.

And you're not paying to travel around Australia doing it.

You're being paid.

As a ShowcasingOz TV Videographer / Video Editor, you'll receive a $1,500 gross weekly contract rate, with your accommodation covered throughout contracted production travel, a daily meal allowance, and admission to the attractions and experiences you're required to film covered as part of the production.

You'll meet locals and hear the stories behind their towns and regions. You'll work with tourism operators, accommodation providers and small business owners. You'll visit places many Australians have never experienced themselves, while building an extraordinarily diverse body of professional work across the country.

For most people, travelling extensively around Australia means saving for years and then watching those savings disappear on accommodation, meals, attractions and experiences.

This opportunity turns much of that equation around.

You'll be earning an income while travelling, with several of the biggest costs of life on the road covered as part of the production.

If you've ever wanted to combine professional filmmaking with seeing and experiencing Australia properly, this is an opportunity that simply doesn't come around very often.

🗺️ This Isn't Just Seeing Australia Through a Windscreen

ShowcasingOz TV isn't simply driving from one major city to another.

Our production is designed around getting out and actually experiencing the destinations we visit. That means exploring the attractions, landmarks, businesses, landscapes and hidden gems that give each town and region its identity.

Across the production, you could find yourself filming:

  • Museums, galleries and cultural attractions
  • Historic sites, heritage buildings and places with remarkable Australian stories
  • Wildlife parks, sanctuaries and animal experiences
  • Adventure attractions and outdoor experiences
  • Wineries, food producers and regional dining experiences
  • Beaches, coastlines, lookouts, waterfalls and spectacular natural landscapes
  • National parks, walking trails and remote points of interest
  • Motels, hotels, caravan parks and unique accommodation
  • Local tours, tourism operators and visitor experiences
  • Restaurants, cafés, bakeries and other small businesses
  • Regional towns, communities and the people who call them home
  • Interviews and conversations with owners, operators and locals
  • Behind-the-scenes and supplementary content for ShowcasingOz TV's digital platforms

Some days will be about capturing breathtaking scenery. Others may involve interviewing a local business owner, exploring an historic attraction, filming an accommodation property or documenting an experience most people would normally have to pay to enjoy on holiday.

The variety is one of the biggest attractions of the role. Your subject, location and working environment will continually change as the production moves around Australia.

📸 Build a Portfolio That's Difficult to Build Any Other Way

Think about what your portfolio could look like after filming across two seasons of ShowcasingOz TV.

Instead of a collection dominated by similar local projects, you'll have the opportunity to develop work spanning tourism, landscapes, accommodation, interviews, businesses, wildlife, attractions, food and wine, adventure, lifestyle, regional storytelling and destination content.

You'll also be working in constantly changing environments, from controlled indoor locations to unpredictable outdoor conditions, busy attractions, remote landscapes and live interactions with people you've only just met.

That variety doesn't just make the role interesting. It provides an opportunity to demonstrate versatility, adaptability and real-world production experience across an unusually broad range of subjects and locations.

🎥 Your Role in the Production

You'll be a key member of a small travelling production crew rather than one person buried inside a large production department.

Your primary responsibility will be capturing high-quality footage throughout the journey while also contributing to editing, media management and the broader production workflow.

At times, our two videographers may split coverage across different locations. You may therefore occasionally be required to film a business, operator, attraction or location independently while the other videographer captures content elsewhere.

That means we're not simply looking for somebody who knows how to operate a camera.

We need someone we can trust to represent ShowcasingOz TV professionally, make good creative decisions in the field and return with footage they're proud to put their name to.

🎥 Required Skills & Experience

  • Proven videography experience supported by a strong portfolio, showreel or examples of previous work
  • Strong understanding of composition, lighting, framing, audio capture and visual storytelling
  • Ability to operate professional video equipment including cameras, gimbals, microphones and lighting equipment
  • Experience shooting with professional cinema or mirrorless camera systems
  • Ability to edit fast-turnaround content using professional editing software such as Adobe Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve
  • Experience with colour grading, audio mixing and exporting content for professional digital distribution
  • Ability to produce clean, stable and visually consistent footage suitable for professional production
  • Confidence filming across constantly changing indoor and outdoor environments
  • Sound knowledge of file management, backups and on-location data handling
  • Ability to coordinate effectively with the second videographer to maintain visual consistency across production
  • Ability to work independently when required while remaining a collaborative member of a small production team
  • Comfortable with extensive travel and extended periods working throughout regional and remote Australia
