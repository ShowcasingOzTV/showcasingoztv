About ShowcasingOz TV

ShowcasingOz TV is an independent Australian travel and lifestyle media project with a clear purpose: to showcase everything that makes Australia worth exploring and encourage more people to experience it for themselves.

From beautiful scenery and exciting attractions to captivating landmarks, welcoming accommodation and amazing hidden gems, we want to capture the places and experiences that inspire people to plan a trip, take a detour, stay another night and discover somewhere new.

Our ambition is to help boost Australian tourism and increase visitor numbers, bookings and revenue for small businesses, accommodation providers and attraction owners. We intend to showcase destinations across the country, with particular attention to regional, rural and remote communities and the local economies that depend on visitors.

Discover. Capture. Share.

Our approach is to Discover, Capture, and Share what each destination has to offer: where to go, what to see, what to do and where to stay. Our planned filming will combine destination coverage, accommodation features, attraction visits and local business showcases.

Australia-wide coverage means making room for the familiar favourites as well as the places travellers may not yet know. Our planned content includes:

  • Major cities, regional centres, country towns and remote communities.
  • Coastlines, national parks, waterfalls, lookouts and spectacular natural scenery.
  • Historic sites, cultural landmarks, museums, galleries and local points of interest.
  • Wildlife encounters, tours, family attractions and outdoor experiences.
  • Hotels, motels, caravan parks and distinctive places to stay.
  • Local food, cafés, wineries, shops and other small businesses.
  • Lesser-known attractions and hidden gems that give visitors a reason to explore further.

The planned video content will be shared through the ShowcasingOz TV website, YouTube and social platforms, helping audiences in Australia and overseas discover more of what this country offers. Our website already provides destination information and accommodation booking links through our Booking.com affiliation.

Helping Visitors Find Their Next Destination

A traveller can only choose a place they know about. An appealing attraction, a comfortable country motel or an unforgettable local experience can be overlooked simply because it has not reached the people who would enjoy it.

We want our content to help close that gap. By showing what visitors can experience in a destination, we aim to turn interest into visits, bookings and local spending. For regional businesses in particular, reaching new customers can make a meaningful difference.

The benefits of a visit can extend beyond a single operator. Guests staying locally may also eat at a café, browse the shops, join a tour or visit another attraction. Encouraging people to explore and stay longer is central to our ambition to support local businesses and economies.

Meet the Founder

ShowcasingOz TV was founded by David Stidston, whose vision brings together a love of Australian travel and a desire to help the businesses and communities that welcome visitors.

David developed the concept around a simple observation: Australia has an extraordinary range of places to discover, yet many small businesses and regional destinations need greater exposure to potential visitors. His aim is to build a professional travel media business that helps put those places in front of a wider audience.

He has devoted extensive time to researching destinations, planning filming routes, developing the website and approaching tourism operators and prospective supporters. His focus remains on turning that groundwork into a sustainable production capable of showcasing Australia across multiple destinations and seasons.

Our Core Values

Authenticity

Present destinations and experiences accurately, with useful information that helps viewers understand what makes a place worth visiting.

Community

Keep local businesses and communities at the heart of our purpose, with a particular focus on helping regional tourism operators reach potential visitors.

Discovery

Encourage people to explore Australia's variety, from celebrated landmarks to overlooked attractions, welcoming towns and unexpected hidden gems.

Where We Are Now

ShowcasingOz TV is currently in pre-production. National filming will commence once sufficient funding and the necessary crew, equipment and travel arrangements are in place. Confirmed dates will be announced when those requirements have been met.

A pilot production focused on one Australian town is also being considered to give viewers and prospective backers a first look at the proposed format. Updates on production plans are available on our Home page.
