Barkly Roadhouse (NT)

Barkly Roadhouse is a remote and important outback stop on the Barkly Highway in the Northern Territory. The destination is now operated as Barkly Homestead and provides essential services for travellers crossing the vast Barkly Tableland between Tennant Creek and the Queensland border.

Its isolated location makes Barkly Homestead an important stopping point for tourists, professional drivers, pastoral workers and other travellers moving through northern Australia. Fuel, meals, refreshments, supplies and overnight accommodation are available in one convenient location.

The surrounding Barkly Tableland is characterised by expansive grasslands, large pastoral properties, distant horizons and dramatic outback skies. Travelling through the region gives visitors a strong appreciation of the scale and remoteness of Australia’s northern interior.

Barkly Homestead has also demonstrated the resilience associated with remote Australian communities and businesses. The facilities were rebuilt following the fire that destroyed the former roadhouse in 2022, allowing this essential highway service to continue supporting travellers and the surrounding region.

Visitors should prepare carefully before travelling through the area. Long distances separate fuel and service locations, while mobile coverage may be limited away from the roadhouse. Travellers should carry sufficient drinking water, monitor fuel levels and check current road and weather conditions before setting out.

For many travellers, Barkly Roadhouse is more than a practical refuelling stop. It offers a genuine encounter with the isolation, hospitality and character of the Australian outback while providing a welcome break during one of the country’s great long-distance road journeys.

Barkly Homestead Big Goannas

The large fighting-goanna sculpture at Barkly Homestead is one of the roadhouse’s most recognisable features and a popular photograph stop for travellers crossing the Barkly Tableland.

Frewena Rest Area

A public highway rest area east of Barkly Homestead where motorists can pause during the long drive across the tableland. Travellers should not rely on the rest area for fuel, accommodation or other commercial services.
