Macclesfield (SA)

Macclesfield is a historic rural township in the southern Adelaide Hills, surrounded by rolling farmland, vineyards, forest country and the upper reaches of the Angas River. Its peaceful setting and strong village character make it an appealing stop between the central Adelaide Hills and the Fleurieu Peninsula.

The township was established during the nineteenth century and retains a collection of historic churches, hotels, cottages and civic buildings. These structures reflect Macclesfield’s early development as a local centre for farming, trade and community life.

Macclesfield is particularly known for its broad tree-lined streets, heritage buildings and community parklands. The Angas River passes through the town, linking its natural setting with reserves, walking areas and public spaces used for local gatherings and events.

The surrounding district supports vineyards, cellar doors, galleries and small creative businesses. Longview Vineyard has become one of the area’s best-known destinations, combining cool-climate wines, dining, events and accommodation within the hills outside the township.

Nearby Meadows provides additional shops, food businesses and heritage attractions, while Prospect Hill preserves the stories of early farming communities through its historical museum and collection of pioneer buildings.

Macclesfield also provides a convenient base for exploring Kuitpo Forest. Its pine plantations, native vegetation and shared-use trails attract walkers, cyclists, horse riders and visitors seeking a quiet outdoor experience within easy reach of the township.

Together, Macclesfield, Meadows, Prospect Hill and Kuitpo form a distinctive part of the southern Adelaide Hills, combining country hospitality, local history, cool-climate wine, creative businesses and accessible forest landscapes.

📍 Attractions In And Around Macclesfield

Macclesfield and the surrounding southern Adelaide Hills offer a mixture of wine, art, forest adventure and local history.

  • Longview Vineyard Located among rolling hills just outside Macclesfield, Longview Vineyard is a family-owned wine estate producing cool-climate wines. Visitors can experience the cellar door, seasonal dining, vineyard scenery, events, day-spa treatments and on-site accommodation.
  • Prospect Hill Historical Museum Situated in the nearby village of Prospect Hill, this community museum occupies a collection of historic buildings along the main street. Its displays preserve machinery, household objects, photographs and memorabilia illustrating the lives of the district’s early farming families and pioneers.
  • The Cheese Factory Studio Gallery Located in Meadows, this former cheese factory has been transformed into an intimate arts and cultural venue. The space supports exhibitions, performances, workshops and community events while preserving the character of one of the town’s distinctive historic buildings.
  • TreeClimb Kuitpo Forest TreeClimb Kuitpo Forest offers elevated adventure courses among the forest canopy. A series of obstacles, platforms and zip-lines provides outdoor challenges for different ages and confidence levels within the landscape of Kuitpo Forest.

🗺️ Landmarks, Points of Interest & Hidden Gems

Explore Macclesfield’s heritage township, community parklands and the historic forest and mining landscapes of the surrounding district.

Almanda Silver Mine Complex Image Coming Soon

Almanda Silver Mine Complex

Located within Scott Creek Conservation Park, the Almanda Silver Mine Complex preserves the remains of nineteenth-century mining activity. Historic shafts, stonework, machinery sites and the Old Almanda Mine reveal the district’s short but significant period of copper and silver mining.

Crystal Lake Park Image Coming Soon

Crystal Lake Park

Crystal Lake Park is one of Macclesfield’s most distinctive community spaces. The reserve incorporates public sculptures, open lawns, established trees and nearby Lord Robinson Park, providing a setting for recreation, community events and quiet walks beside the town.

Kuitpo Forest Trail Image Coming Soon

Kuitpo Forest Trail

The shorter Forest Trail introduces visitors to Kuitpo’s mixture of commercial pine plantation and native woodland. Beginning near the Chookarloo area, the trail provides an accessible forest walk beneath tall trees and through changing vegetation.

Macclesfield Town Centre Image Coming Soon

Macclesfield

Macclesfield’s town centre retains the appearance of a traditional Adelaide Hills village, with historic buildings arranged along broad, tree-lined streets. Notable sites include St John’s Anglican Church and the Three Brothers Arms Hotel, an historic public house dating from the town’s early development.

Mackereth Cottage Image Coming Soon

Mackereth Cottage

The remains of Mackereth Cottage stand near Scott Creek at Dorset Vale and preserve part of the district’s early European settlement history. George and Sarah Ann Mackereth established their home here in 1839 and raised their family on the surrounding property. Although only ruins remain, the site provides an important connection with the lives of the area’s earliest farming families.

Meadows Township Image Coming Soon

Meadows

Meadows is a neighbouring rural township serving the surrounding dairy, grazing and farming district. Its town centre includes the historic Memorial Hall, local shops, community facilities and the well-known Meadows Bakery.

Mount Bold Reservoir Lookout Image Coming Soon

Mount Bold Reservoir Lookout

Mount Bold Reservoir Lookout provides expansive views across South Australia’s largest reservoir and the surrounding hills, forests and protected catchment. The reserve supports extensive areas of native vegetation and wildlife, with walking trails allowing visitors to experience the landscape from several vantage points. Access conditions and opening times should be checked before travelling.

Tinjella Trail Image Coming Soon

Tinjella Trail – Christmas Hill Forest

The Tinjella Trail is a marked shared-use loop through the Kuitpo Forest network. Beginning on Christmas Hill Road, the trail passes through pine plantation and native forest and is used by walkers, cyclists and horse riders exploring the quieter parts of the reserve.
