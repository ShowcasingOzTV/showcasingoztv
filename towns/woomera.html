Woomera (SA)

Woomera is a distinctive outback community in northern South Australia, located approximately six kilometres north of Pimba and around 450 kilometres from Adelaide. Surrounded by broad arid plains, salt lakes and defence lands, the town occupies a unique place in Australia’s military, scientific and aerospace history.

The township was established in the late 1940s to support the Anglo-Australian weapons-testing program and the vast Woomera Rocket Range. Its remote location, clear skies and enormous testing area made it suitable for missile development, weapons trials, high-altitude research and early space-related programs. Thousands of defence personnel, scientists, technicians and their families lived in Woomera during its busiest years.

Woomera’s planned streets, purpose-built facilities and surviving operational relics reflect its origins as a specialised government settlement. The town’s museums and displays interpret projects that involved Australia, Britain and other international partners, while Rocket Park presents aircraft, missiles and equipment connected with decades of testing and technological development.

The surrounding district adds another dimension to the visitor experience. Lake Hart reveals the stark beauty of a vast salt-lake landscape, while Pimba remains an essential highway service point. Former tracking and defence sites, pastoral ruins and distant settlements illustrate the extraordinary scale and isolation of this part of South Australia.

Woomera remains an active defence-support community as well as a public township. Visitors can explore accessible heritage displays and public areas, but should observe all signs and access restrictions associated with nearby defence land and former operational facilities.
