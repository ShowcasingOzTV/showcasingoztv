Normanville & Yankalilla (SA)

Normanville is a relaxed seaside town on South Australia’s Fleurieu Peninsula, located beside a long sandy beach approximately 75 kilometres south of Adelaide. Together with neighbouring Yankalilla, Carrickalinga and Lady Bay, the town forms an important visitor destination along the Fleurieu Coast.

The Normanville district developed during the nineteenth century as agriculture, grazing, mining and coastal trade expanded across the southern Fleurieu Peninsula. Normanville’s beach and former jetty provided a connection between the district’s farms and coastal shipping, while Yankalilla became an inland service centre for surrounding rural communities.

Normanville is now known for its accessible foreshore, village atmosphere, cafés, accommodation and proximity to several of the peninsula’s most attractive beaches. Normanville Beach stretches between Carrickalinga to the north and Lady Bay to the south, offering opportunities for swimming, walking, fishing and coastal photography.

Nearby Yankalilla retains a strong historical identity through its churches, museum collections, older buildings and long association with the surrounding agricultural district. The Fleurieu Coast Visitor Centre provides local travel information while sharing its site with the Yankalilla District Historical Museum.

South of Normanville, Lady Bay and Wirrina Cove combine coastal scenery with resort facilities, golf and elevated viewpoints. Farther along the coast, Second Valley is recognised for its historic settlement, sheltered beach, jetty, rock formations and clear water popular with swimmers, snorkellers, divers and fishers.

The district provides a convenient base for exploring a broad section of the Fleurieu Coast without extending into destinations more appropriately associated with Myponga, Victor Harbor or Cape Jervis. Visitors can combine beaches, heritage, scenic drives, local businesses and outdoor activities within a relatively compact coastal region.

Other Attractions In And Around Normanville

  • Christ Church Yankalilla and Shrine of Our Lady of Yankalilla

    Christ Church is a historic Anglican church in Yankalilla and the home of the Shrine of Our Lady of Yankalilla. The shrine became a place of Christian pilgrimage after an image resembling the Virgin Mary was observed on an interior wall during the 1990s.

  • Fleurieu Coast Visitor Centre and Yankalilla District Historical Museum

    This combined visitor and heritage facility provides information about local attractions, accommodation, events and touring routes. Its historical displays and research collection preserve stories, photographs and objects connected with Normanville, Yankalilla and the surrounding Fleurieu Coast.

  • The Links Lady Bay Golf Course

    The Links Lady Bay is an 18-hole championship golf course set between rolling hills and the Fleurieu coastline. The course forms part of the Lady Bay resort precinct and provides expansive views towards the coast and surrounding countryside.

Carrickalinga Beach

Carrickalinga Beach is known for its pale sand, clear water and uninterrupted views across Gulf St Vincent. Extending north from Normanville, the beach provides a peaceful setting for swimming, walking, fishing and coastal photography.

Carrickalinga Lookout

Carrickalinga Lookout provides an elevated perspective over Carrickalinga Beach, Gulf St Vincent and the surrounding Fleurieu coastline. It is a popular roadside stop for coastal sightseeing, photography and sunset views.

HMAS Hobart Memorial Lookout

The HMAS Hobart Memorial Lookout commemorates the former guided-missile destroyer HMAS Hobart, which was deliberately sunk in Yankalilla Bay to create an artificial reef and recreational dive site. Interpretive displays explain the vessel’s history and identify its offshore resting place.

Lady Bay

Lady Bay is a coastal area immediately south of Normanville, near the mouth of the Yankalilla River. Its beach, dunes, surrounding hills and views across Gulf St Vincent form the southern end of the Normanville coastal precinct.

Image Coming Soon

Normanville Beach

Normanville Beach is a long sandy beach extending between Carrickalinga and Lady Bay. Its foreshore and jetty precinct provide opportunities for swimming, walking, fishing, family recreation and enjoying views across Gulf St Vincent.

Normanville Town Centre

Normanville Town Centre contains local cafés, shops, visitor services and historic buildings along Main Road. Its compact layout, community atmosphere and proximity to the beachfront contribute to the town’s relaxed coastal character.

Second Valley

Second Valley is a small historic settlement south of Normanville, surrounded by rural hills and dramatic coastal scenery. Its surviving stone buildings and quiet streets reflect the early European development of the southern Fleurieu Peninsula.

Second Valley Beach and Jetty

Second Valley Beach is a sheltered coastal destination known for its clear water, rocky shoreline, historic boatsheds and jetty. The precinct is popular for swimming, snorkelling, recreational diving, fishing and exploring the coastline.

Yankalilla

Yankalilla is an historic inland township that serves the surrounding agricultural district and neighbouring coastal communities. Its main street contains heritage buildings, churches, community facilities, shops and visitor services.
