Melrose (SA)

Melrose is a historic country town positioned beneath Mount Remarkable in South Australia’s Southern Flinders Ranges. Surrounded by mountain slopes, native bushland and agricultural country, it serves as an important southern gateway to the broader Flinders Ranges region.

Established during the nineteenth century, Melrose is regarded as the oldest town in the Flinders Ranges. The discovery of copper helped drive its early development, while farming, transport and services for the surrounding district contributed to the town’s long-term survival.

Melrose retains an attractive collection of stone buildings, hotels, cottages and civic structures. These buildings preserve connections with the town’s mining, brewing, policing and agricultural history and give the central streets a strong heritage character.

Mount Remarkable rises immediately beside the township and reaches approximately 960 metres above sea level. The mountain and surrounding national park provide habitat for native wildlife and extensive opportunities for bushwalking, scenic viewing and nature-based exploration.

The town has also developed a national reputation as a mountain-biking destination. Trail networks extend through the surrounding foothills and bushland, attracting riders of different abilities and supporting events, businesses and accommodation connected with outdoor recreation.

Nearby Wilmington provides additional services, accommodation and attractions for travellers exploring the Southern Flinders Ranges. The town is also a gateway to Alligator Gorge and the northern sections of Mount Remarkable National Park.

Today, Melrose combines its historic identity with cafés, accommodation, cycling, walking and community events. Its position beneath Mount Remarkable makes it a memorable base for exploring the landscapes, heritage and small communities of the Southern Flinders Ranges.

📍 Attractions In And Around Melrose

Melrose and nearby Wilmington offer visitors local history, heritage buildings and small community attractions connected with the Southern Flinders Ranges.

  • Jacka Brothers Brewery The historic Jacka Brothers Brewery complex preserves an important part of Melrose’s commercial history. Brewing began at the site during the nineteenth century, and its stone buildings remain a prominent local landmark. The restored complex now provides a place for visitors to experience its architecture, gardens, hospitality and connection with the Jacka family.
  • Melrose Heritage Museum Located within the town’s historic former courthouse and police station precinct, Melrose Heritage Museum presents objects, photographs and displays connected with local settlement, policing, mining, agriculture and community life. The surviving buildings and former holding cells form an important part of the museum experience.
  • Wilmington Toy Museum The Wilmington Toy Museum displays a wide-ranging collection of toys, models and memorabilia assembled across many decades. Its collection provides a nostalgic look at changing childhood interests, manufacturing styles and popular culture, making it an unusual attraction within the Southern Flinders Ranges.

Doughboy Reserve

Doughboy Reserve is a roadside recreation area in the wider Melrose district, providing a quiet place to stop among the rural landscapes of the Southern Flinders Ranges. The reserve is used for picnics, short breaks and appreciating the surrounding bushland and agricultural scenery.

Goyder’s Monument Image Coming Soon

Goyder’s Monument

Goyder’s Monument recognises South Australian Surveyor-General George Goyder and his influential assessment of the boundary between reliable agricultural country and more drought-prone land. The monument reflects the importance of rainfall, surveying and land management to the settlement of the region.

Mount Remarkable Summit Trail Image Coming Soon

Mount Remarkable Summit Trail and Soldiers Memorial Monument

The Mount Remarkable Summit Trail climbs from Melrose through changing vegetation towards the summit of the mountain. The challenging walk rewards experienced and prepared hikers with elevated views across the Southern Flinders Ranges and surrounding plains.

The Soldiers Memorial Monument adds an important commemorative element to the mountain landscape, honouring local service personnel and preserving the district’s connection with Australia’s wartime history.
