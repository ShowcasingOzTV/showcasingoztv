Finley (NSW)

Finley is a regional town in the southern Riverina of New South Wales, located within Berrigan Shire near the junction of the Newell and Riverina highways. Its position along these important transport routes has helped establish Finley as a service centre for surrounding farms, smaller communities and travellers moving through the region.

The town stands on Yorta Yorta Country and is surrounded by an agricultural landscape shaped by generations of farming and water management. European settlement expanded across the district during the nineteenth century, with pastoral activity followed by closer settlement, transport infrastructure and the development of irrigation.

The arrival of the railway in 1898 strengthened Finley’s connections with regional markets and supported the movement of passengers, livestock and agricultural produce. Finley’s surviving pioneer railway station and museum now preserve this important part of the town’s transport history.

Irrigation transformed Finley and the wider district into part of one of Australia’s most productive food-growing regions. Rice, grain, dairy, livestock and other agricultural enterprises have all contributed to the local economy. The network of channels, water infrastructure and farming properties surrounding the town demonstrates how closely Finley’s development is tied to the availability and management of water.

Local museums preserve different aspects of Finley’s history. The railway museum documents the development of regional transport, the historical museum and log cabin explore pioneering rural life, and the foundry museum records the district’s manufacturing and agricultural engineering heritage.

Lake Finley provides an attractive recreational space near the centre of town. Its walking track, open surroundings and opportunities for waterside recreation give residents and visitors a place to exercise, relax and enjoy the landscape. Local shops, hospitality venues, sporting clubs and community facilities add to Finley’s practical and welcoming country character.

Explore attractions filming & feature options

  • Finley Foundry Museum Located within the original J.T. and E.N. Close foundry premises, this museum explores more than a century of local manufacturing and agricultural engineering. Its collection includes historic machinery, water carts, wagons, farming equipment, tools and business records connected with the foundry’s long association with the district.
  • Finley Historical Museum & Log Cabin This community museum preserves objects, machinery and buildings associated with the pioneering families of the Finley district. Its displays include a replica log cabin based on the area’s first permanent residence and the former Myrtle Park school building, providing insight into rural life before Federation.
  • Finley Railway Museum Housed within Finley’s well-preserved pioneer railway station, the museum contains railway artefacts, memorabilia and stories dating from the arrival of the railway in the late nineteenth century. The precinct demonstrates the economical pioneer station design once used to extend rail services into regional areas.

Finley Town Centre

Finley Town Centre brings together local shops, cafés, hospitality venues, professional services and community facilities. Its broad streets and mixture of commercial and heritage buildings reflect the town’s continuing role as a service centre for the surrounding agricultural district.

Lake Finley

Lake Finley is a popular local recreation area with a scenic 1.8-kilometre walking and cycling track around the water. Visitors can enjoy the lakeside surroundings, picnic areas and activities such as canoeing and boating when conditions permit.
