Roxby Downs, South Australia

Roxby Downs is a modern outback town in South Australia’s Far North, approximately 560 kilometres north of Adelaide. Built among red sand dunes and arid plains, the town provides residential, commercial and community services for the nearby Olympic Dam mining operation and the surrounding district.

The town lies on the traditional lands of the Kokatha people, whose cultural connection with this part of northern South Australia continues today. The surrounding landscape forms part of a much broader cultural environment with enduring importance to Kokatha families and communities.

Roxby Downs was developed during the 1980s following the discovery and development of the Olympic Dam ore body. Unlike many Australian mining towns that grew gradually, Roxby Downs was purposefully planned with residential neighbourhoods, schools, sporting facilities, parks and a central commercial precinct.

Olympic Dam contains substantial copper, uranium, gold and silver resources and has played a major role in the town’s development. Although the mine is central to the regional economy, it is an operating industrial site and does not offer general public mine tours.

Roxby Downs is also closely associated with arid-zone conservation. Arid Recovery operates a large fenced reserve near the town, protecting native wildlife and supporting research into the restoration of Australian desert ecosystems.

Visitors can explore the Emu Walking Trail, learn about Olympic Dam through the presentation at the visitor centre and experience the distinctive wildlife and landscapes of the region. Roxby Downs provides an unusual combination of contemporary town planning, mining history, conservation and remote outback life.

🎟️ Attractions In And Around Roxby Downs

Arid Recovery Sunset Tour

Join a guided experience within the Arid Recovery Reserve to learn about arid-zone conservation, ecological research and the reintroduction of locally extinct native animals. Tour availability can be seasonal and advance bookings may be required.

📍 Landmarks, Points Of Interest & Hidden Gems

Emu Walk

Follow a three-kilometre circuit through Roxby Downs, passing residential areas, the community garden, sand dunes and nature reserves. Interpretive signs introduce the town, local plants, wildlife and surrounding landscape. Collecting a current trail map is recommended because some directional markers may be missing.

Olympic Dam

Olympic Dam is a major underground mining operation containing copper, uranium, gold and silver resources. Public mine tours are not offered, but visitors can learn about its history and operations through the Olympic Dam video presentation at the Roxby Downs Visitor Information Centre.

Purple Downs Station

Purple Downs is a working pastoral station within the wider Roxby Downs region and forms part of the area’s continuing cattle industry. It is not a general public attraction, so visitors must respect private property, station operations and any access restrictions.

Red Lake Tracking Station

This former tracking facility south of Roxby Downs is associated with the wider history of technical and defence activity in the region. The site is not presented as a developed visitor attraction, and access should not be assumed without confirming land status, road conditions and relevant permissions.
