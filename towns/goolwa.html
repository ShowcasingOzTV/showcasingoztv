Goolwa (SA)

Goolwa is a historic river-port town on South Australia’s Fleurieu Peninsula, positioned near the lower reaches of the Murray River, the Coorong and the Southern Ocean. Its waterfront, maritime heritage and relaxed atmosphere give the town a strong connection to both river and coast.

Visitors can explore heritage streetscapes, boating experiences, beaches, local dining and the surrounding wetlands and waterways. Goolwa also provides a convenient base for discovering nearby coastal communities and other destinations across the southern Fleurieu Peninsula.
