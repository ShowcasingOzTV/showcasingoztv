Goolwa (SA)

Goolwa is a historic river-port town on Ngarrindjeri Country at the southern end of South Australia’s Fleurieu Peninsula. Positioned near the final reaches of the Murray River, the Murray Mouth, Lake Alexandrina and the Coorong, Goolwa occupies one of the most environmentally and culturally significant waterways in Australia.

During the nineteenth century, Goolwa developed as an important inland port connecting river traffic with coastal transport. Paddle steamers carried wool, grain and other goods along the Murray-Darling river system, while the railway between Goolwa and Port Elliot provided a link between the river and the sea. Much of this history remains visible around the Goolwa Wharf Precinct.

The river continues to shape the town’s identity. Cruises, paddling experiences, marinas, historic vessels and waterfront businesses provide different ways to explore the lower Murray. The nearby Goolwa Barrage forms part of the system separating the fresh water of the Lower Lakes from the tidal waters extending towards the Murray Mouth and Coorong.

Goolwa is also closely connected with the coast. Goolwa Beach and nearby Middleton Beach are known for surfing, walking, fishing and seasonal whale watching. The Encounter Bikeway links coastal communities through a shared trail used by cyclists and walkers.

This page also covers Middleton, Hindmarsh Island and Currency Creek. Middleton contributes surf culture, beaches, historic structures and local artisan businesses. Hindmarsh Island provides access to marinas, farm experiences, the barrage and views towards the Murray Mouth. Currency Creek adds vineyards, railway heritage, historic bridges and peaceful rural scenery.

Goolwa’s town centre combines heritage buildings with cafés, galleries, accommodation, visitor services and local businesses. Community organisations, museums and cultural spaces preserve the stories of the river port while new tourism and hospitality businesses continue to strengthen the destination.

📍 Attractions In And Around Goolwa

Attractions featured on ShowcasingOz TV are captured through immersive 4K video storytelling and professional photography, showcasing local experiences, culture, history and visitor engagement in a way that static listings cannot achieve.

Explore attractions filming & feature options

  • Art@Goolwa Inc A community-focused arts organisation and gallery showcasing work by local and regional artists. Exhibitions and creative activities help visitors engage with the artistic character of Goolwa and the wider Fleurieu Peninsula.
  • Canoe the Coorong Guided paddling experiences that introduce visitors to the waterways, sand dunes, wildlife, ecology and cultural significance of the Coorong. Tours provide a low-impact way to explore areas that are best experienced from the water.
  • Goolwa Animal Farm A family-oriented attraction where visitors can meet and interact with a variety of farm animals. The property offers animal-feeding experiences, open spaces and activities designed to introduce children to rural animals and farm life.
  • Goolwa National Trust Museum Located in a former blacksmith’s shop dating from around 1872, the museum presents the history of Goolwa, Currency Creek and Hindmarsh Island. Its collection covers river trade, local industries, settlement, community life and the development of the district.
  • Goolwa Visitor Information Centre Located within the Signal Point Experience Centre, Goolwa Visitor Services provides local maps, destination information and guidance on attractions, cruises, beaches, events and experiences across the Alexandrina region and Fleurieu Peninsula.
  • Hindmarsh Island Marina & Islanders’ Tavern A marina precinct on Hindmarsh Island providing moorings, marine services and access to the lower Murray waterways. Islanders’ Tavern adds waterfront dining and a relaxed meeting place overlooking the marina.
  • Kombi Surf A locally operated surf school providing lessons and coaching at Middleton Beach. Its experiences help beginners and developing surfers build confidence while learning about ocean conditions and surf safety.
  • Middleton Chocolates A local Middleton business producing handmade chocolates, truffles, creams, fudge and house-made ice cream. It provides visitors with an opportunity to sample locally made confectionery while exploring the coastal township.
  • Narnu Farm A family farm experience on Hindmarsh Island where visitors can encounter farm animals and take part in activities connected with traditional rural life. The property is particularly known for hands-on experiences designed for families and groups.
  • One Paddock Currency Creek Winery A winery and cellar-door destination within the Currency Creek wine region. Visitors can explore locally produced wines while experiencing the vineyard landscape and cool-climate character of the district.
  • PS Oscar W Cruise A restored historic paddle steamer operating from Goolwa Wharf. Scheduled cruises offer a living-history experience and allow passengers to see the lower Murray from a vessel connected with Australia’s river-trading era.
  • River Port Heritage Centre Located within the historic Goolwa Wharf Precinct, the centre interprets the town’s development as a major Murray River port. Its displays explore paddle-steamer trade, rail transport, local industry and the people who worked along the river.
  • Shaw Family Vintners A family-owned wine producer with vineyards, winemaking facilities and a cellar door at Currency Creek. Visitors can taste wines from several labels while learning about the region’s cool-climate viticulture.
  • Signal Point Experience Centre An interpretive and cultural centre overlooking the Murray River at Goolwa Wharf. Its exhibition explores Ngarrindjeri and colonial heritage together with the ecology of the Murray River, Lower Lakes and Coorong.
  • Spirit of the Coorong Cruises & Coorong National Park Cruises departing from Goolwa Wharf explore the lower Murray, Murray Mouth and Coorong. Guided experiences provide commentary on the region’s ecology, wildlife, cultural heritage and changing waterways, with selected cruises including guided walks within Coorong National Park.
  • Steam Exchange Brewery A small independent brewery located within the historic Goolwa Wharf Precinct. Visitors can sample handcrafted beer in a setting closely connected with the town’s river-port and railway heritage.

This list is provided for visitor awareness only. Inclusion does not imply partnership or endorsement. Opening hours, tours and access arrangements should be confirmed directly with each operator before visiting.

🗺️ Landmarks, Points of Interest & Hidden Gems

These landmarks and local points of interest form an important part of Goolwa, Middleton, Hindmarsh Island and Currency Creek. ShowcasingOz TV will capture these locations during filming to help tell the broader story of the region.

Aboriginal Canoe Tree Image Coming Soon

Aboriginal Canoe Tree

A culturally significant scarred tree showing where bark was carefully removed to create a canoe or another traditional object. The site provides a physical reminder of the long Ngarrindjeri connection with the lands and waterways of the lower Murray.

Basham Beach Image Coming Soon

Basham Beach

A broad coastal beach and conservation area between Middleton and Port Elliot. Its exposed shoreline, dunes and elevated viewing areas are popular for walking, coastal scenery and observing southern right whales during their seasonal migration.

Blue Temper Ironworks & Middleton Station Image Coming Soon

Blue Temper Ironworks & Middleton Station

Blue Temper Ironworks showcases the work of Middleton blacksmith and artist Chris Murphy near the town’s heritage railway station. The area connects contemporary metalwork with Middleton’s railway history and includes the artist’s distinctive public sculptures.

Currency Creek Image Coming Soon

Currency Creek

A small rural township and waterway north of Goolwa where vineyards, wetlands, railway infrastructure and historic sites come together. The surrounding district forms part of the recognised Currency Creek wine region.

Currency Creek Lions Park & Waterfall Image Coming Soon

Currency Creek Lions Park & Waterfall

A peaceful public reserve beside the creek with picnic facilities, shady vegetation and access to the nearby waterfall. Water flow varies with seasonal rainfall, so the character of the site changes throughout the year.

Currency Creek Railway Viaduct Image Coming Soon

Currency Creek Railway Viaduct

An imposing historic railway structure spanning the Currency Creek valley. The viaduct demonstrates the engineering required to establish rail connections across the southern Fleurieu Peninsula and remains a prominent feature of the local landscape.

Encounter Bikeway Image Coming Soon

Encounter Bikeway

A shared coastal pathway connecting Goolwa with Middleton and communities farther west. The route gives cyclists and walkers access to beaches, coastal viewpoints, reserves and sections of the region’s historic railway corridor.

Finniss Image Coming Soon

Finniss

A small rural settlement north of Goolwa surrounded by grazing properties, farmland and open Fleurieu countryside. Finniss contributes another perspective on the agricultural communities connected with Currency Creek and the lower Murray district.

Goolwa Barrage Image Coming Soon

Goolwa Barrage

One of the structures built across channels near the Murray Mouth to manage the boundary between the fresh water of the Lower Lakes and the tidal waters of the Coorong. The barrage is also a useful location for observing waterbirds and the surrounding waterways.

Goolwa Beach Image Coming Soon

Goolwa Beach

A long and exposed ocean beach popular for surfing, fishing, walking and four-wheel-drive access towards the Murray Mouth. Visitors driving on the beach must assess current sand, tide and weather conditions before entering.

Goolwa Railway Station Image Coming Soon

Goolwa Railway Station

A heritage railway station associated with the historic rail connection established to move goods between the Murray River and the coast. It remains part of the SteamRanger heritage railway experience and Goolwa’s transport story.

Goolwa Town Centre Image Coming Soon

Goolwa Town Centre

The commercial and community heart of Goolwa, combining heritage buildings, shops, cafés, galleries, accommodation and everyday services. Its streets reflect the town’s transition from working river port to modern regional and tourism centre.

Goolwa Wharf Precinct Image Coming Soon

Goolwa Wharf Precinct

Goolwa’s principal heritage and waterfront precinct, bringing together the wharf, historic vessels, railway, visitor centre, museum experiences, galleries, hospitality businesses and river cruises.

Hindmarsh Island Bridge Image Coming Soon

Hindmarsh Island Bridge

The bridge linking Goolwa with Hindmarsh Island across the Goolwa Channel. It provides an elevated view of the waterway and is an important transport connection for island residents, businesses and visitors.

Historic Currency Creek Cemetery Image Coming Soon

Historic Currency Creek Cemetery

A small historic burial ground containing graves connected with early settlers and families of the Currency Creek district. The surviving memorials provide insight into the community’s colonial history and changing population.

Historic Middleton Mill Ruin Image Coming Soon

Historic Middleton Mill Ruin (Bowman’s Mill)

The remains of an early flour mill associated with Middleton’s nineteenth-century development. The surviving stonework recalls the importance of grain production, milling and local industry in the early settlement period.

Middleton Image Coming Soon

Middleton

A coastal township between Goolwa and Port Elliot known for its surf culture, heritage railway, local businesses and access to a long section of the southern Fleurieu coastline.

Middleton Beach Image Coming Soon

Middleton Beach

A well-known surfing beach with a long sandy shoreline and consistent ocean exposure. It is used for surfing lessons, swimming when conditions allow, beach walks and seasonal whale watching.

Murray Mouth Lookout & Sugars Beach Image Coming Soon

Murray Mouth Lookout & Sugars Beach

A Hindmarsh Island viewing area overlooking the channels, dunes and shifting sands near the Murray Mouth. Sugars Beach provides a convenient base from which visitors can observe the meeting point of the river system and Southern Ocean.

Old Currency Creek Road Bridge Image Coming Soon

Old Currency Creek Road Bridge

A surviving piece of early road infrastructure associated with travel through the Currency Creek district. The bridge contributes to the collection of railway, road and settlement heritage found throughout the creek valley.
