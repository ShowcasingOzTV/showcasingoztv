Griffith (NSW)

Griffith is a major regional city in the Riverina of southern New South Wales. Surrounded by vineyards, orchards, rice fields and broad agricultural properties, the city sits at the centre of one of Australia’s most productive food and wine regions.

Griffith was established during the development of the Murrumbidgee Irrigation Area in the early twentieth century. Its street plan was designed by Walter Burley Griffin and Marion Mahony Griffin, whose work also influenced the design of Canberra. The planned city developed as irrigation channels transformed semi-arid country into productive farmland.

The irrigation system supported intensive agriculture and attracted settlers from many cultural backgrounds. Italian migration had a particularly strong influence on Griffith’s identity, contributing to local viticulture, farming, food production, hospitality and community life.

Griffith is now recognised for its wineries, cellar doors, restaurants, fresh produce and food-manufacturing industries. The surrounding region produces wine grapes, citrus, rice, vegetables, poultry and other agricultural products distributed throughout Australia and overseas.

The city’s museums, memorials and heritage sites document irrigation, migration, agriculture and wartime history. Public art, galleries, festivals and cultural organisations reflect the diversity of the community and the continuing contribution of people from many different backgrounds.

Beyond the city centre, Scenic Hill, Hermit’s Cave and Lake Wyangan provide opportunities to explore the local landscape. Smaller communities including Binya, Coleambally, Goolgowi, Rankins Springs, Whitton and Yenda contribute additional agricultural, cultural and historical experiences across the wider district.

📍 Attractions In And Around Griffith

Attractions featured on ShowcasingOz TV are captured through immersive 4K video storytelling and professional photography, showcasing local experiences, history, culture and visitor engagement in a way that static listings cannot achieve.

Explore attractions filming & feature options

  • Altina Wildlife Park A wildlife park near Darlington Point offering guided animal-viewing experiences. Visitors can encounter native and exotic species while learning about animal care, conservation and breeding programs.
  • Berton Vineyards A winery based in Yenda producing a broad collection of Riverina and Australian wines. The winery reflects the scale and importance of viticulture within the Griffith district.
  • Binya Hall A community hall serving the small rural locality of Binya. The building reflects the importance of local gathering places within dispersed agricultural communities.
  • Calabria Family Wines A family-owned Griffith winery shaped by generations of Italian winemaking experience. Its cellar door introduces visitors to locally produced wines and the family traditions behind the business.
  • Catania Fruit Salad Farm A family-operated farm experience near Hanwood where visitors can learn about local horticulture, irrigation, farm production and the contribution of Italian families to the Griffith district.
  • Darlington Point Museum A community museum preserving photographs, objects and stories connected with Darlington Point and the surrounding Murrumbidgee district.
  • De Bortoli Wines One of the Riverina’s best-known family wine businesses, with a cellar door near Griffith. Visitors can explore a wide range of wines while learning about the De Bortoli family’s Italian heritage and regional winemaking history.
  • Griffith Italian Museum and Cultural Centre A cultural centre documenting the experiences and contributions of Italian migrants who helped shape Griffith’s farming, food, wine, business and community identity.
  • Griffith Pioneer Park Museum A large open-air museum preserving historic buildings, machinery, vehicles and collections associated with the development of Griffith and the Murrumbidgee Irrigation Area.
  • Griffith Regional Art Gallery A public gallery presenting exhibitions by regional and visiting artists. Its program supports visual arts, touring exhibitions and community participation.
  • Griffith Tourism Hub Griffith’s visitor information centre provides maps, local knowledge and advice about accommodation, wineries, attractions, events and experiences throughout the wider region.
  • Griffith War Memorial Museum A volunteer-supported museum preserving military objects, photographs and personal stories connected with local servicemen, servicewomen and their families.
  • Made To Thrive A Griffith-based creative and community enterprise showcasing locally made products, workshops and activities designed to support wellbeing, skills and social connection.
  • Southern Cotton Gin Tour A guided experience at Whitton explaining how locally grown cotton is processed after harvest. Tours provide insight into modern agricultural technology and one of the region’s major industries.
  • The Aisling Distillery A local distillery producing handcrafted spirits in Griffith. Visitors can learn about the distilling process and sample products created using a combination of traditional methods and regional influences.
  • Wally’s Junk Art Gallery A distinctive Rankins Springs attraction featuring imaginative sculptures and artworks created from discarded machinery, metal and other recycled materials.
  • Whitton Malt House A regional food, drink and visitor destination at Whitton connected with the production and interpretation of malting grains. The venue combines agricultural education with dining and recreation.
  • Yarran Wines A family-owned winery near Yenda producing wines from Riverina fruit. Its cellar door provides an opportunity to explore contemporary local winemaking and vineyard views.
  • Yarrow Park Cellar Door A Coleambally-area cellar door showcasing wines produced from local vineyards and the agricultural conditions of the wider Riverina.

This list is provided for visitor awareness only. Inclusion does not imply partnership or endorsement. Tours, tastings and opening hours should be confirmed directly with each operator before visiting.
