Stawell (VIC)

Stawell is a historic regional town in western Victoria, located at the northern gateway to the Grampians National Park. Known for its gold mining heritage, sporting traditions and strong community identity, Stawell plays an important role as a commercial and service centre for the surrounding Wimmera district.

The town developed during the Victorian gold rush after gold was discovered in the district during the 1850s. Mining attracted thousands of people to the area and shaped Stawell’s early economy, streetscape and population. Historic buildings, former mining areas and continuing mining operations reflect the town’s long association with the industry.

Stawell is widely recognised as the home of the Stawell Gift, one of Australia’s oldest and most prestigious professional foot races. Held at Central Park over the Easter long weekend, the event attracts athletes, spectators and media from across Australia and forms a major part of the town’s sporting identity.

The town also provides access to significant Aboriginal cultural heritage. Bunjil’s Shelter, located within the wider district, contains important rock art depicting Bunjil, a creator being within the cultural traditions of Aboriginal peoples across parts of Victoria.

Stawell’s location places it between Victoria’s western agricultural plains and the rugged landscape of the Grampians. Nearby bushland, lookouts, parks, wineries and historic communities provide visitors with opportunities to explore the wider region while using Stawell as a practical base.

Today, Stawell offers retail services, accommodation, community facilities, public parks and heritage attractions. Its combination of gold-mining history, sporting culture, regional art and proximity to the Grampians gives the town a distinctive place within western Victoria.

📍 Attractions

Stawell and nearby Great Western offer visitors a combination of wineries, galleries, sporting history, local museums and gold-mining heritage.

Explore attractions filming & feature options

  • Best’s Wines – Great Western A historic Great Western winery producing regional wines from vineyards with a long connection to western Victoria’s winemaking history.
  • Grampians Estate Winery – Great Western A regional winery offering visitors an introduction to wines produced from vineyards across the Grampians wine region.
  • Grampians Time Gallery A specialist gallery displaying an extensive collection of clocks, watches and timepieces representing different periods, styles and approaches to mechanical timekeeping.
  • Seppelt Winery – Great Western One of Great Western’s best-known wineries, with a long history of regional wine production and extensive underground cellars known as the Drives.
  • St Joseph’s Catholic Church – Glenorchy A historic church connected with the development of Glenorchy and the religious life of the surrounding rural community.
  • Stawell Gift Hall of Fame A collection celebrating the history of the Stawell Gift, its notable competitors and the development of professional foot racing in Australia.
  • Stawell Gold Mine A continuing part of Stawell’s gold-mining industry and a major contributor to the town’s modern economy. Public access and visitor opportunities depend on current operating arrangements.
  • Stawell Historical Society A community organisation preserving photographs, records, objects and stories connected with Stawell’s mining, civic and social history.
  • Stawell Railway Station Gallery A community gallery within Stawell’s historic railway station precinct, presenting local and regional creative work in a heritage setting.

Central Park
