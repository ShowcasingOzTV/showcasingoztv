Halls Gap (VIC)

Halls Gap is a picturesque village situated in the heart of the Grampians, or Gariwerd, in western Victoria. Surrounded by sandstone mountain ranges, forested valleys and abundant native wildlife, the village is the principal visitor base for exploring one of Victoria’s most recognisable natural landscapes.

The surrounding country holds deep cultural significance for the Djab Wurrung and Jardwadjali peoples. Gariwerd contains an exceptional concentration of Aboriginal cultural places, creation stories and rock-art sites. Brambuk provides visitors with an important introduction to the living culture, history and enduring connection Traditional Owners have with this landscape.

Halls Gap developed as a tourism and service centre because of its location inside the mountain valley. Visitors can travel directly from the village to walking tracks, waterfalls, rocky gorges and elevated viewpoints. Several popular walks also begin close to the town centre, making it possible to experience the landscape without travelling great distances.

Waterfalls are among the region’s best-known natural features. MacKenzie Falls flows throughout the year, while seasonal locations such as Clematis Falls, Splitters Falls, Bridal Veil Falls and Turret Falls are most impressive following suitable rainfall. Water levels, walking conditions and track access can vary substantially throughout the year.

Elevated destinations such as Boroka Lookout, Reed Lookout, The Balconies, The Pinnacle and Mount William provide very different views across the ranges, valleys and western Victorian plains. These places range from accessible roadside lookouts to more demanding mountain walks that require appropriate footwear, preparation and weather awareness.

The wider Halls Gap area also includes family attractions, Aboriginal culture, wildlife experiences, galleries, wineries, local produce and small tourism businesses. Nearby Pomonal, Lake Fyans, Great Western and Wartook broaden the range of experiences and accommodation available to visitors using Halls Gap as their base.

📍 Attractions In And Around Halls Gap

Halls Gap and the surrounding Grampians region offer Aboriginal cultural experiences, wildlife attractions, galleries, wineries, local produce and family activities. These businesses and experiences form an important part of the region’s visitor economy.

Explore attraction filming & feature options

Brambuk, The National Park & Cultural Centre

Brambuk introduces visitors to the Aboriginal culture, stories and landscapes of Gariwerd. Its exhibitions and cultural information explain the continuing relationship Traditional Owners have with the mountains and surrounding country. The centre also provides a valuable starting point for understanding the national park before exploring its walks and cultural places.

Fallen Giants Vineyard

Fallen Giants Vineyard is a family-run winery near Halls Gap, positioned beneath the Grampians ranges. The vineyard produces cool-climate wines that reflect the local soils and growing conditions. Tastings allow visitors to experience regional wine while enjoying views towards the surrounding mountains.

Five Ducks Farm

Five Ducks Farm is a small regional producer near Pomonal, specialising in preserves and products made using locally grown ingredients. The business reflects the productive side of the Grampians region, where orchards, gardens and small farms operate beneath the mountain ranges.

Grampians Adventure Golf, Café, Bar & MOCO Gallery

Grampians Adventure Golf combines an extensively landscaped miniature-golf course with a café, licensed bar and art-gallery experience. The course draws inspiration from the surrounding Grampians landscape and provides an accessible activity for families, couples and groups visiting Halls Gap.

Halls Gap Zoo

Halls Gap Zoo is a privately operated regional wildlife park near the village. It is home to native and exotic animal species and provides opportunities for visitors to learn about animal care, conservation and wildlife from Australia and overseas. The zoo is one of the area’s major family attractions.

Pomonal Estate

Pomonal Estate is a winery, brewery and hospitality venue located near the eastern edge of the Grampians. Visitors can sample locally produced wine and beer, enjoy meals and take in views towards the ranges. The property connects regional produce with the natural setting surrounding Pomonal.

Smugglers Hearth

Smugglers Hearth is a local Halls Gap business offering visitors another opportunity to explore the village’s range of independent retail and hospitality experiences. Its inclusion helps highlight the small businesses that contribute to the character and visitor economy of the town.

Steve Morvell Art for Conservation

Steve Morvell Art for Conservation showcases detailed wildlife artwork inspired by animals, conservation and the natural world. The artist’s work combines creative practice with environmental awareness, making the gallery particularly relevant to visitors exploring a region known for its wildlife and national-park landscapes.

Wildlife Art Museum of Australia

The Wildlife Art Museum of Australia, commonly known as WAMA, is a developing cultural institution near Halls Gap dedicated to art, science, nature and conservation. Its exhibitions and programs explore relationships between people, wildlife and the environment within a setting closely connected to the Grampians landscape.

Attraction opening hours, tours and tasting experiences may change. Confirm current availability directly with each business before travelling. Inclusion does not imply a commercial partnership or endorsement.

🗺️ Landmarks, Points of Interest & Hidden Gems

Halls Gap provides access to mountain lookouts, waterfalls, walking tracks, historic places and neighbouring communities throughout the central Grampians. Conditions can change following storms, bushfires and park-management works, so visitors should check current advice before entering the national park.

Boroka Lookout Image Coming Soon

Boroka Lookout

Boroka Lookout provides expansive views across Halls Gap, Lake Bellfield, the Fyans Valley and the surrounding ranges. Its proximity to the car park makes it one of the most accessible elevated viewpoints in the central Grampians.

Bridal Veil Falls Image Coming Soon

Bridal Veil Falls

Bridal Veil Falls is a seasonal cascade encountered along the Wonderland walking route towards The Pinnacle. Following rainfall, water falls beside the track near Silent Street and adds another natural feature to this rugged mountain walk.

Burrong Falls Image Coming Soon

Burrong Falls

Burrong Falls is a seasonal waterfall surrounded by forest and rocky Grampians terrain. It offers a quieter alternative to the national park’s busiest waterfall locations and is best viewed when recent rain has produced a reliable flow.

Chautauqua Peak Image Coming Soon

Chautauqua Peak

Chautauqua Peak rises above Halls Gap and can be reached by a forested walking circuit from near the village. The upper sections become steeper and rockier, rewarding prepared walkers with elevated views across the valley and surrounding ranges.

Clematis Falls Image Coming Soon

Clematis Falls

Clematis Falls is a seasonal waterfall reached by a walking track from the Halls Gap area. The route passes through native vegetation and provides a convenient short walk when rainfall has brought the waterfall to life.

Fish Falls Image Coming Soon

Fish Falls

Fish Falls is located downstream from MacKenzie Falls along the MacKenzie River. The walking route follows the gorge through rocky and forested terrain, leading to a broad cascade that is generally quieter than its better-known neighbour.

Grand Canyon Image Coming Soon

Grand Canyon Loop Walk

The Grand Canyon forms part of the Wonderland walking area near Halls Gap. The route passes between high sandstone walls, boulders and narrow rocky passages, providing an immersive introduction to the geology of the central Grampians.

Halls Gap Botanic Gardens Image Coming Soon

Halls Gap Botanic Gardens

Halls Gap Botanic Gardens provide a peaceful community space featuring plants associated with the Grampians and surrounding region. The gardens offer visitors an accessible opportunity to observe local flora close to the village centre.

Halls Gap Town Centre Image Coming Soon

Halls Gap Town Centre

Halls Gap Town Centre is the principal visitor hub for the central Grampians. Cafés, restaurants, shops, accommodation and visitor services sit beside Stony Creek beneath the surrounding sandstone ranges.

Kalymna Falls Image Coming Soon

Kalymna Falls

Kalymna Falls is a seasonal waterfall in the Mount William area of the Grampians. Reached by a bushwalking track, the falls descend over a rocky overhang and are most impressive following periods of suitable rainfall.

Lake Bellfield Image Coming Soon

Lake Bellfield & Fyans Creek Dam Wall

Lake Bellfield is a scenic reservoir immediately south of Halls Gap, framed by the Mount William and Wonderland ranges. The Fyans Creek dam wall and nearby viewing areas provide broad views over the water and surrounding mountains.

MacKenzie Falls Image Coming Soon

MacKenzie Falls & Lookout

MacKenzie Falls is one of Victoria’s largest and best-known waterfalls. Water descends over a substantial cliff into the gorge below, while the lookout provides an alternative view for visitors who do not undertake the steep walk to the base.

Mount William Image Coming Soon

Mount William

Mount William, traditionally known as Duwul, is the highest point in the Grampians. The exposed summit provides far-reaching views across the ranges and western plains, although conditions can be considerably colder and windier than in Halls Gap.

Pomonal Town Centre Image Coming Soon

Pomonal Town Centre

Pomonal is a small rural community east of Halls Gap, surrounded by orchards, vineyards, farmland and views towards the Grampians. Its local businesses and community facilities provide another perspective on life around the national park.

Reed Lookout Image Coming Soon

Reed Lookout

Reed Lookout offers elevated views across Victoria Valley, Victoria Range and the surrounding Grampians landscape. It also serves as the starting point for the walking track leading to The Balconies.

Silverband Falls Image Coming Soon

Silverband Falls

Silverband Falls is named for the narrow ribbon of water that descends over its rock face. A relatively short forest walk leads visitors to the base, with the strongest flow generally occurring after seasonal rainfall.

Splitters Falls Image Coming Soon

Splitters Falls

Splitters Falls is a seasonal waterfall located within walking distance of Halls Gap. The track follows Stony Creek through forest and rocky terrain, making it a rewarding village-based walk when water is flowing.

The Balconies Image Coming Soon

The Balconies

The Balconies are distinctive sandstone formations reached by a walking track from Reed Lookout. Designated viewing areas provide sweeping views across Victoria Valley and the western ranges without requiring visitors to enter closed or unsafe areas.

The Pinnacle Image Coming Soon

The Pinnacle Lookout

The Pinnacle is one of the central Grampians’ best-known lookouts. Several walking routes lead to the exposed sandstone viewpoint, which overlooks Halls Gap, Lake Bellfield and the Fyans Valley.

Turret Falls Image Coming Soon

Turret Falls

Turret Falls is a seasonal waterfall reached through forested terrain near Halls Gap. The falls are less heavily visited than several major Grampians sites and provide a quieter destination when conditions are suitable.

Venus Baths Image Coming Soon

Venus Baths Loop

The Venus Baths Loop follows Stony Creek from Halls Gap to a series of naturally formed rock pools. Smooth sandstone, seasonal water and easy access from town make it one of the area’s most popular shorter walks.

Zumsteins Image Coming Soon

Zumsteins Park & Historic Area

Zumsteins preserves the remains and stories of an early twentieth-century tourist settlement beside the MacKenzie River. Interpretive material, historic structures and the surrounding landscape provide insight into the development of tourism in the Grampians.

Waterfall flow, walking-track access and road conditions vary. Temporary closures may occur because of fire, flood, storm damage or maintenance. Check current Parks Victoria advice before travelling.
