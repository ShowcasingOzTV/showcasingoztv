Myponga (SA)

Myponga is a small rural town on South Australia’s Fleurieu Peninsula, situated among rolling farmland between the Mount Lofty Ranges and the coast. Located approximately 60 kilometres south of Adelaide, it provides a convenient base for exploring Myponga Reservoir, Myponga Beach, Mount Compass, Sellicks Hill and the southern Fleurieu countryside.

The surrounding district has long been associated with agriculture, including dairy farming, grazing, orchards and berry production. Its productive rural landscape continues to support local growers, food producers and visitor experiences that showcase the produce and agricultural character of the Fleurieu Peninsula.

Myponga Reservoir is one of the area’s defining landmarks. Constructed across the Myponga River, the reservoir supplies drinking water to parts of the Fleurieu Peninsula. Public access initiatives have also allowed visitors to enjoy designated walking and cycling trails, reservoir views and the surrounding natural landscape.

The nearby coastline adds another dimension to the region. Myponga Beach is a secluded coastal settlement set between steep hills and the sea, while Sellicks Beach and Silver Sands Beach offer broad coastal views, exposed cliffs and distinctive geological features.

Myponga and Mount Compass also provide access to breweries, orchards, berry farms, local food experiences and country hospitality. Together, these destinations combine agricultural production, natural scenery and small-business experiences within a relatively compact section of the Fleurieu Peninsula.

📍 Attractions

Myponga and its surrounding communities offer a varied collection of local food, beverage, cultural and hospitality experiences shaped by the agricultural and coastal character of the Fleurieu Peninsula.

Explore attractions filming & feature options

  • Ceravolo Berries A Fleurieu Peninsula berry producer connected with the region’s productive agricultural landscape. The business grows and supplies fresh berries while contributing to the local food and farming industries surrounding Myponga and Mount Compass.
  • Harvest the Fleurieu – High Tea A regional produce destination at Mount Compass offering a high-tea experience showcasing locally grown and locally sourced ingredients. The venue also connects visitors with the farming traditions and seasonal produce of the Fleurieu Peninsula.
  • Kerrie’s Cherries A family-operated cherry-growing business in the Pages Flat district. The orchard reflects the diversity of the Fleurieu Peninsula’s agricultural industries and provides visitors with an opportunity to discover locally grown seasonal fruit.
  • Nan Hai Pu Tuo Temple of Australia A prominent Buddhist temple complex at Sellicks Hill overlooking the surrounding coastal and rural landscape. The temple provides a place for worship, reflection and cultural connection within a peaceful hillside setting.
  • Smiling Samoyed Brewery An independent brewery in Myponga overlooking the reservoir. Visitors can sample locally produced beer, enjoy meals and potentially see the brewing process while meeting the resident Samoyeds that helped inspire the business’s name.
  • The Blueberry Patch A seasonal berry-growing and farm-gate destination near Mount Compass. The business highlights locally produced blueberries and the favourable growing conditions found within this part of the Fleurieu Peninsula.
  • Victory Hotel A long-established dining destination at Sellicks Hill with expansive views across Sellicks Beach, Aldinga Bay and the Fleurieu coastline. The hotel is particularly known for its restaurant, extensive wine cellar and scenic coastal outlook.

Mount Compass

A rural service town surrounded by dairy farms, grazing country, orchards and wetlands. Mount Compass provides local shops, hospitality businesses and access to several food and agricultural experiences within the central Fleurieu Peninsula.

Myponga Beach

A secluded coastal settlement where steep hills descend towards a sheltered beach and rocky shoreline. Its quiet setting and coastal views contrast with the farming country surrounding Myponga township.

Myponga Reservoir Lookout

A scenic stopping point providing elevated views across Myponga Reservoir and the surrounding hills. The lookout helps visitors appreciate the scale of the reservoir and its position within the Fleurieu Peninsula landscape.

Myponga Town Centre

A compact rural township containing local businesses, community facilities and connections with the surrounding agricultural district. The town centre provides a practical stopping point between the Adelaide region and the southern Fleurieu Peninsula.

Rock Bridge Image Coming Soon

Rock Bridge – Sellicks Beach

A naturally formed coastal rock feature near Sellicks Beach, created through the continuing effects of erosion and wave action. The formation contributes to the geological interest and rugged character of this section of coastline.

Silver Sands Beach

A broad sandy beach south of Aldinga known for its open coastal views and long shoreline. The beach forms part of the transition between Adelaide’s southern coastline and the more rural landscapes of the Fleurieu Peninsula.
