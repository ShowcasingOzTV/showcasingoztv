Milang, South Australia

Lakeside heritage, railway history and peaceful waterfront discoveries beside beautiful Lake Alexandrina.

Discover Milang

Milang is a historic lakeside town situated on the western shore of Lake Alexandrina, approximately 80 kilometres south-east of Adelaide. Once an important river port serving paddle steamers and surrounding farming communities, the town retains a strong connection to the transport, agricultural and maritime history of the Lower Murray region.

Before the development of extensive railway networks and modern roads, Milang became a busy transfer point for goods travelling across Lake Alexandrina and along the Murray River system. Produce from surrounding farms was brought into town before being transported by vessel, while supplies arriving at the port were distributed throughout the district. The surviving jetty, hand crane and railway heritage help tell the story of this once-bustling commercial centre.

Today, Milang offers visitors a quieter experience shaped by open lake views, historic buildings and a close-knit community. Its compact town centre makes it easy to explore local heritage sites, community attractions and the foreshore while enjoying the relaxed atmosphere of a small South Australian country town.

The Milang Railway Museum preserves the story of the railway line that once connected the town with Strathalbyn and the broader South Australian rail network. Exhibits, restored railway equipment and the former station precinct provide an engaging introduction to the role transport played in Milang’s growth.

Another significant part of the town’s history can be found at the Milang Lakeside Butter Factory. Dating from the late nineteenth century, the restored complex reflects the importance of dairy farming and local food production to the surrounding district. Its preservation has been driven by considerable community effort.

Nearby Clayton Bay extends the Milang experience to another peaceful waterfront community. Located beside the waterways connecting Lake Alexandrina with the lower Murray system, Clayton Bay is known for its foreshore, boating environment, wetland scenery and abundant birdlife.

Together, Milang and Clayton Bay provide a rewarding base for visitors interested in regional history, railway heritage, lakeside scenery, fishing, boating and the natural environment of the Lower Lakes.

🎟️ Attractions In And Around Milang

Milang Lakeside Butter Factory

Originally established in 1893, the Milang Lakeside Butter Factory reflects the importance of dairy farming and food production in the district. The restored heritage complex contains historic equipment, photographs and displays exploring the factory’s operation, the surrounding farming community and Milang’s commercial history.

Milang Railway Museum

Located within Milang’s former railway station precinct, the museum preserves the history of the railway that once connected the town with Strathalbyn. Its collection includes railway memorabilia, restored equipment, historical displays and interactive experiences illustrating the development of rail transport in South Australia.

Clayton Bay & Foreshore

Clayton Bay is a peaceful waterfront settlement surrounded by waterways, wetlands and bird habitat. Its foreshore provides attractive views, picnic areas and access to a landscape popular with boaters, anglers, paddlers and nature lovers.

Clayton Bay Compass

This distinctive foreshore feature identifies surrounding directions and destinations while helping visitors understand Clayton Bay’s position within the Lower Lakes and Murray River waterways. It also provides an interesting stopping point during a walk along the waterfront.

Milang Jetty Hand Crane & Lake Alexandrina

The historic hand crane near Milang Jetty recalls the period when the town operated as an important lake port. Set against the broad waters of Lake Alexandrina, the site connects Milang’s surviving industrial heritage with one of South Australia’s most significant freshwater environments.

Milang Town Centre

Milang’s town centre retains the relaxed character of a historic rural service town. Heritage buildings, community facilities and local businesses provide a starting point for exploring the railway precinct, lakeside foreshore and nearby historical sites.
