Warrnambool (VIC)

Warrnambool is a major coastal city in south-west Victoria, positioned near the western end of the Great Ocean Road and overlooking the Southern Ocean. Its combination of beaches, rocky headlands, maritime history, regional services and established visitor infrastructure makes it one of the principal destinations along Victoria’s Shipwreck Coast.

The Eastern Maar people are the Traditional Owners of the Warrnambool region, with enduring cultural connections to the coastline, waterways and volcanic landscapes. European settlement expanded during the nineteenth century as the natural harbour supported shipping and trade, while the surrounding Western District developed through agriculture, dairying and related industries.

Warrnambool’s coastal identity remains central to its visitor appeal. Logan’s Beach is recognised for seasonal southern right whale viewing, while Thunder Point, Pickering Point, Stingray Bay and the foreshore provide walking routes and broad views of the exposed coastline. Flagstaff Hill Maritime Village interprets the shipping history and shipwreck stories that gave this section of coast its enduring name.

The surrounding district adds volcanic and rural landscapes to the coastal experience. Tower Hill occupies an ancient volcanic crater west of the city, while Hopkins Falls, Mortlake, Koroit and smaller farming communities reveal different aspects of the region’s natural and cultural history. Warrnambool itself provides extensive shopping, dining, accommodation, arts, recreation and transport services.

📍 Attractions

Warrnambool and its surrounding district offer maritime history, arts, wildlife encounters, family activities, regional food and geothermal bathing experiences.

Explore attractions filming & feature options

  • Cheese World & Museum An Allansford visitor attraction celebrating the district’s dairy industry through local produce, cheese tastings and historical displays.
  • Clarke's Pies - Mortlake A long-running Mortlake bakery known for its pies, pastries and connection with the town’s local food traditions.
  • Deep Blue Hot Springs A geothermal bathing sanctuary offering mineral-rich pools and wellness experiences near Warrnambool’s coastal foreshore.
  • Flagstaff Hill Maritime Village A recreated nineteenth-century maritime precinct interpreting the ships, industries and human stories associated with the Shipwreck Coast.
  • Fletcher Jones Market A market and retail precinct occupying part of the historic Fletcher Jones factory complex and its surrounding heritage gardens.
  • Mini Golf By The Sea A family-friendly mini-golf attraction near Warrnambool’s foreshore and Lake Pertobe recreation precinct.
  • Mount Shadwell Quarry - Mortlake A volcanic quarry experience associated with Mortlake’s geology and the discovery of olivine and other minerals within the ancient volcanic landscape.
  • Warrnambool Art Gallery A regional gallery presenting historical and contemporary art through its permanent collection, changing exhibitions and public programs.
  • Warrnambool Wildlife Encounters A guided wildlife experience introducing visitors to Australian animals through close-up educational encounters.
