Tibooburra (NSW)

Tibooburra is one of New South Wales’ most remote towns, situated in the far north-west of the state approximately 330 kilometres north of Broken Hill. Surrounded by granite outcrops, red earth and expansive plains, it serves as the principal service centre for Corner Country and an important base for travellers exploring Sturt National Park.

The town emerged during the Albert Goldfields rush of the 1880s, when prospectors travelled into the district in search of gold. Although the mining boom was relatively brief, it contributed to the establishment of government services, hotels, businesses and permanent buildings. Several surviving structures and mining sites continue to reveal this early chapter.

Tibooburra’s name is commonly interpreted as meaning “heap of rocks” or “place of stones”, reflecting the rounded granite formations surrounding the town. These weathered outcrops give the landscape its distinctive appearance and provide elevated places from which visitors can watch the colours of the country change at sunrise and sunset.

Pastoralism became another defining part of the district, with large stations operating across country affected by drought, extreme heat and highly variable rainfall. Historic machinery, station buildings, memorials and former transport routes illustrate the practical skills and resilience required to live and work in this isolated environment.

Today, Tibooburra supports residents, pastoral properties, National Parks operations and long-distance travellers. Historic hotels, local museums, visitor facilities and accommodation businesses provide a welcome stop before journeys through Sturt National Park, towards Cameron Corner or south through Milparinka and the wider Corner Country region.

📍 Attractions

Tibooburra and the surrounding Corner Country offer museums, cultural places, historic buildings and locally operated visitor stops that reveal the region’s mining, pastoral and Aboriginal heritage.

Explore attractions filming & feature options

  • Courthouse Museum A historic courthouse presenting displays connected with Tibooburra’s mining, pastoral, social and natural history.
  • Milparinka Museum A museum within the historic Milparinka precinct preserving stories of the Albert Goldfields, early settlement and life in Corner Country.
  • Outdoor Pastoral Museum An open-air collection of machinery and equipment illustrating the practical history of pastoral work in the surrounding district.
  • Packsaddle Roadhouse A long-established roadside business providing fuel, food and a welcome stopping point for people travelling through far-western New South Wales.
  • Tibooburra Local Aboriginal Land Council Keeping Place A local cultural place supporting the preservation and sharing of Aboriginal heritage, knowledge and community stories from the region.
