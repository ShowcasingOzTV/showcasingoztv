Silverton, New South Wales

Silverton is a historic outback village approximately 25 kilometres northwest of Broken Hill in far-western New South Wales. Its surviving colonial buildings, desert setting and creative community preserve the atmosphere of a settlement that once stood at the centre of a major mining rush.

The district developed after silver-bearing ore was discovered during the 1870s and early 1880s. Silverton rapidly grew into a busy mining settlement with hotels, businesses, government buildings and thousands of residents drawn by the possibility of work and mineral wealth.

The discovery of the much larger ore body at Broken Hill shifted mining, investment and population away from Silverton. Many buildings were dismantled or abandoned, leaving behind a small community surrounded by ruins, former mine workings and reminders of the town’s brief period of rapid expansion.

Silverton later developed a second identity through art, tourism and filmmaking. Artists were drawn to the clarity of the light and the colours of the surrounding landscape, while filmmakers recognised the visual power of the village, Mundi Mundi Plains and Barrier Ranges.

Productions filmed in and around Silverton include Australian films, television programs, advertisements and music videos. Its strongest screen association is with Mad Max 2, whose desert filming locations helped establish the region as an internationally recognised cinematic landscape.

Today, visitors can explore private galleries, museums, heritage buildings, a working visitor mine, camel experiences and classic outback hospitality. Beyond the village, lookouts, creek beds, historic water infrastructure and expansive plains reveal the scale and distinctive character of far-western New South Wales.

🎟️ Attractions In And Around Silverton

Justin Cowz Ghost Town Art Gallery

Visit a privately operated gallery presenting the distinctive work of Silverton artist Justin Cowz. The collection draws inspiration from outback characters, abandoned places and the landscapes of far-western New South Wales.

Historic Daydream Mine

Join a guided underground tour through a former silver mine and learn about the demanding conditions experienced by nineteenth-century miners. Tour schedules vary seasonally, so bookings and operating times should be confirmed before travelling.

John Dynon Gallery

Discover original outback artwork created by Silverton artist John Dynon. His colourful and expressive paintings capture local characters, desert landscapes and the humour associated with life in the Australian outback.

Mad Max 2 Museum

Explore a privately operated museum dedicated to Mad Max 2. Its displays include photographs, memorabilia, costumes, life-sized figures, original material and replica vehicles connected with the film and its production around Silverton.

Silverton Gaol and Historical Museum

Step inside the former 1889 gaol to explore extensive collections relating to Silverton and Broken Hill. Displays cover mining, household life, transport, medicine, local organisations, music and the experiences of people who lived across the district.

Silverton Hotel

Visit one of Silverton’s best-known businesses for meals, drinks, film memorabilia and traditional outback hospitality. The historic hotel has appeared in numerous films, television productions, advertisements and photographic campaigns.

Silverton Outback Camels

Book a camel ride or farm visit and experience the Silverton landscape through a locally operated tourism business. Available experiences can include shorter rides, sunset tours and opportunities to meet animals at the property.

Silverton Photography Gallery & Garden

Browse photographic work inspired by Silverton, its residents and the surrounding desert environment, then explore the adjoining garden and creative spaces associated with the gallery.

Silverton School Educational Museum

Visit Silverton’s former school and discover the history of education in the district. The museum preserves classroom furnishings, photographs, records and teaching material, with educational visits available by arrangement.

📍 Landmarks, Points Of Interest & Hidden Gems

Creek Bed

Explore the visual character of Silverton’s dry creek country, where exposed earth, river red gums and erosion patterns demonstrate how occasional water flows shape the otherwise arid landscape.

Mad Max 2 Replica Compound

View a reconstruction inspired by the fortified compound featured in Mad Max 2. The structure reflects Silverton’s continuing connection with the film and is located on private tourism property, so access conditions and opening arrangements must be respected.

Mundi Mundi Lookout

Take in sweeping views across the Mundi Mundi Plains from this renowned roadside lookout. The immense horizon, changing light and open desert country make it one of the region’s most recognisable landscape viewpoints.

Silverton Cemetery

Visit a significant historic cemetery containing hundreds of identified grave sites. Its headstones record the short lives, family histories and difficult conditions experienced during Silverton’s mining era.

Silverton Donkeys

Silverton’s free-roaming donkeys have become familiar local characters and are frequently seen around the village. Visitors should observe them carefully, avoid obstructing roads and follow local guidance regarding interaction or feeding.

Umberumberka Reservoir

See an important part of the district’s historic water-supply system, developed to support Silverton and later Broken Hill in an environment where reliable water was essential. Access and viewing conditions should be checked before visiting.
