Karatta (SA)

Karatta is a sparsely populated rural locality on the western side of Kangaroo Island, surrounded by native bushland, farmland and some of the island’s most dramatic coastal scenery. Rather than having a concentrated town centre, the locality extends across a broad landscape near Flinders Chase National Park, Kelly Hill Conservation Park and Hanson Bay.

The western end of Kangaroo Island is known for its rugged Southern Ocean coastline, wind-shaped vegetation, limestone formations and important wildlife habitat. Karatta provides a practical base for visitors exploring this less-developed part of the island and its internationally recognised natural landmarks.

Flinders Chase National Park is central to the area’s visitor appeal. Its coastal environment contains Remarkable Rocks, Admirals Arch, the Cape du Couedic Lighthouse and the historic remains at Weirs Cove, together with walking routes, lookouts and opportunities to observe native wildlife.

The Karatta district was heavily affected by the 2019–20 Kangaroo Island bushfires. The fires changed large areas of vegetation, damaged visitor infrastructure and affected local wildlife and businesses. Regrowth, rebuilding and conservation work have since become an important part of the region’s continuing story.

Beyond Flinders Chase, the area includes the limestone cave environments of Kelly Hill, the sheltered shoreline of Hanson Bay and remote beaches exposed to the conditions of the Southern Ocean. Wildlife experiences throughout the district may include kangaroos, wallabies, echidnas, koalas, birdlife and colonies of long-nosed fur seals.

Karatta’s accommodation and visitor services are limited compared with Kangaroo Island’s larger settlements. However, its holiday park cabins and luxury lodge accommodation provide access to the island’s western wilderness without requiring visitors to base themselves closer to Kingscote, Parndana or Vivonne Bay.

📍 Attractions

Karatta provides access to wildlife encounters, cave experiences, visitor information and family activities across the western side of Kangaroo Island.

Explore attractions filming & feature options

  • Flinders Chase Visitor Centre The principal visitor information point for Flinders Chase National Park, helping travellers understand current park conditions, available facilities and the natural and cultural features of Kangaroo Island’s western end. Visitors should check park alerts and access information before travelling into remote areas.
  • Hanson Bay Wildlife Sanctuary A privately operated wildlife sanctuary near Hanson Bay where visitors can experience regenerating bushland and learn about native animals, conservation and the landscape’s recovery following the Kangaroo Island bushfires.
  • Kangaroo Island Mini Golf A family-friendly recreational attraction in the Karatta area offering a relaxed activity for visitors staying near Flinders Chase National Park and the western Kangaroo Island attractions.
  • Kelly Hill Caves A limestone cave system within Kelly Hill Conservation Park, formed by water moving through the island’s limestone landscape. Access and tour arrangements can change, so visitors should check current conditions and booking requirements before travelling.

🗺️ Points of Interest & Landmarks

Western Kangaroo Island contains remote beaches, historic lighthouse infrastructure, geological formations and coastal lookouts shaped by the powerful conditions of the Southern Ocean.

Admirals Arch and Fur Seal Lookout Image Coming Soon

Admirals Arch & Fur Seal Lookout

A naturally formed coastal arch at Cape du Couedic reached by a boardwalk through a rugged shoreline environment. Nearby viewing areas provide opportunities to observe long-nosed fur seals resting and moving among the rocks below.

Bunker Hill Lookout Image Coming Soon

Bunker Hill Lookout

An elevated viewing point within the western Kangaroo Island landscape, providing a perspective across regenerating bushland and the extensive natural environment surrounding Flinders Chase.

Cape du Couedic Lighthouse Image Coming Soon

Cape du Couedic Lighthouse

A historic stone lighthouse completed in the early twentieth century to help guide shipping around Kangaroo Island’s hazardous south-western coastline. It remains one of the most recognisable structures in Flinders Chase National Park.

Hanson Bay Beach Image Coming Soon

Hanson Bay Beach

A picturesque south-coast beach bordered by native vegetation and exposed to changing Southern Ocean conditions. The bay forms part of the remote coastal landscape between Kelly Hill and Flinders Chase.

Remarkable Rocks Image Coming Soon

Remarkable Rocks

A group of immense granite formations sculpted by weathering over millions of years. Their distinctive shapes and elevated coastal setting have made them one of Kangaroo Island’s most recognisable natural landmarks.

Scott Cove Lookout Image Coming Soon

Scott Cove Lookout

A coastal viewpoint overlooking the cliffs, coves and open waters of Kangaroo Island’s western coastline. The lookout demonstrates the scale and isolation of this remote Southern Ocean landscape.

Weirs Cove Historic Ruins and Lookout Image Coming Soon

Weirs Cove Historic Ruins & Lookout

The remains of buildings and infrastructure once used to land supplies for the Cape du Couedic lighthouse keepers. The site also provides commanding views over the cliffs and Southern Ocean.

West Bay Beach Image Coming Soon

West Bay Beach

A remote beach on the western side of Kangaroo Island surrounded by protected wilderness. Reaching the area requires careful planning, and visitors should check current road, weather and park conditions before setting out.
