American River (SA)

American River is a peaceful coastal township on the western shore of Eastern Cove on Kangaroo Island. Known locally as “The River”, the community overlooks sheltered water and the entrance to Pelican Lagoon, creating a setting recognised for birdlife, boating, fishing, seafood and a relaxed maritime character.

The area lies within the traditional Country of the Ngarrindjeri and Kaurna-related peoples associated with Kangaroo Island and the adjacent mainland. Its waterways, coastal vegetation and marine habitats form part of a much longer cultural and environmental story than the township itself.

The name American River dates to 1803, when a party of American sealers working from the brig Union stayed in the inlet and built the schooner Independence. The vessel is widely recognised as the first ship constructed in South Australia. This maritime history remains an important part of the town’s identity and is interpreted through the community-led Rebuild Independence Project.

Today, the town’s jetty, wharf and waterfront precinct remain closely connected with recreational fishing, aquaculture and local seafood. Nearby Pelican Lagoon Marine Sanctuary protects a complex tidal environment of islands, seagrass beds and sheltered channels that support abundant birdlife and marine species.

American River is positioned between Penneshaw and Kingscote, making it a useful base for exploring eastern and central Kangaroo Island. Coastal walking trails, maritime heritage, farm-gate produce and nearby locations including Prospect Hill, Pennington Bay and Red Banks give visitors several ways to experience the district. 🗺️ Points of Interest & Landmarks

These coastal landmarks, walking trails and heritage sites reflect American River’s maritime history, protected waterways and connection with the wider Kangaroo Island landscape.

American River Maritime Enclave Image Coming Soon

American River Maritime Enclave

The waterfront precinct incorporates The Deck Café, American River jetty and the wharf. It is a focal point for boating, fishing, local dining and views across the sheltered waters of Eastern Cove.

Fish Cannery Walking Trail Image Coming Soon

Fish Cannery Walking Trail

This coastal trail follows part of American River’s shoreline towards the site associated with the former fish cannery. The walk combines local history with water views, coastal vegetation and opportunities to observe birdlife.

Independence Point Image Coming Soon

Independence Point

Reached via the Independence Trail, this quiet coastal point looks across American River’s sheltered waters. The route follows the lagoon shoreline through an area valued for its scenery, birdlife and maritime associations.

Mercure Kangaroo Island Lodge Interpretive Trail Image Coming Soon

Mercure Kangaroo Island Lodge Interpretive Trail

This short interpretive trail passes through bushland near the lodge and introduces visitors to the local environment, native vegetation, wildlife habitat and views around American River Bay.

Pelican Lagoon Marine Sanctuary Image Coming Soon

Pelican Lagoon Marine Sanctuary

Pelican Lagoon is a protected tidal lagoon connected to Eastern Cove. Its sheltered waters, islands, seagrass habitat and abundant birdlife make it one of the area’s most important natural environments. Visitors should observe sanctuary rules and protect its sensitive habitat.

Pennington Bay Beach Image Coming Soon

Pennington Bay Beach

Pennington Bay is a striking south-coast beach known for turquoise water, powerful surf, rocky headlands and elevated coastal views. Conditions can be strong, so visitors should take care around the water and use designated access areas.

Prospect Hill Lookout & Matthew Flinders Monument Image Coming Soon

Prospect Hill Lookout & Matthew Flinders Monument

A steep stair climb leads to panoramic views over Pelican Lagoon and Pennington Bay. The site commemorates Matthew Flinders’ 1802 ascent of the hill while surveying the Kangaroo Island coastline.

Rebuild Independence Project Boathouse Image Coming Soon

Rebuild Independence Project Boathouse

Located near the entrance to the American River wharf, the community-run boathouse preserves local maritime history and documents the construction of a replica of the 1803 schooner Independence.

Red Banks Image Coming Soon

Red Banks

Red Banks is a secluded coastal location distinguished by red cliffs, pale sand and views across the water. The contrast between the eroded shoreline and surrounding coastal landscape makes it a memorable scenic stop near American River.
