American River (SA)

American River is a peaceful coastal township on the western shore of Eastern Cove on Kangaroo Island. Known locally as “The River”, the town overlooks sheltered waters and Pelican Lagoon, creating an environment recognised for birdlife, boating, fishing and relaxed coastal living.

The town takes its name from American sealers who stayed in the area in 1803 and built the schooner Independence, recognised as the first vessel built in South Australia. This maritime history remains an important part of the community’s identity and is being preserved through the Rebuild Independence Project.

American River provides access to coastal walking trails, scenic lookouts, sheltered waterways and local food producers. Its position between Penneshaw and Kingscote also makes it a convenient base for exploring the eastern and central areas of Kangaroo Island.

ShowcasingOz TV will capture the places, businesses and stories that define American River, highlighting its maritime heritage, natural environment, local produce and enduring connection to the waters of Eastern Cove and Pelican Lagoon.

These coastal landmarks, walking trails and heritage sites form an important part of American River’s landscape, history and sense of place. ShowcasingOz TV will capture these locations during filming to help tell the broader story of the town and surrounding region.

American River Maritime Enclave

The waterfront precinct brings together the American River jetty, wharf and The Deck Café. It is a focal point for boating, fishing, local dining and views across the sheltered waters of Eastern Cove.

Fish Cannery Walking Trail

This coastal trail follows part of American River’s shoreline towards the site associated with the former fish cannery. The walk combines local history with water views, coastal vegetation and opportunities to observe birdlife.

Independence Point

Reached via the Independence Trail, this quiet coastal point looks across American River’s sheltered waters. The route follows the lagoon shoreline through an area valued for its scenery, birdlife and maritime associations.

Mercure Kangaroo Island Lodge Interpretive Trail

This short interpretive trail passes through bushland near the lodge and introduces visitors to the local environment. It provides an accessible opportunity to experience native vegetation, wildlife habitat and views around American River Bay.

Pelican Lagoon Marine Sanctuary

Pelican Lagoon is a protected tidal lagoon connected to Eastern Cove. Its sheltered waters, islands, seagrass habitat and abundant birdlife make it one of the area’s most important natural environments. Visitors should observe sanctuary rules and protect its sensitive habitat.

Image Coming Soon

Pennington Bay Beach

Pennington Bay is a striking south-coast beach known for its turquoise water, powerful surf, rocky headlands and elevated coastal views. Conditions can be strong, so visitors should take care around the water and enjoy the scenery from designated access areas.

Image Coming Soon

Prospect Hill Lookout & Matthew Flinders Monument

A steep stair climb leads to panoramic views over Pelican Lagoon and Pennington Bay. The site commemorates Matthew Flinders’ 1802 ascent of the hill while surveying the Kangaroo Island coastline.

Image Coming Soon

Rebuild Independence Project Boathouse

Located near the entrance to the American River wharf, the community-run boathouse preserves local maritime history and documents the construction of a replica of the 1803 schooner Independence.

Image Coming Soon

Red Banks

Red Banks is a secluded coastal location distinguished by its red cliffs, pale sand and views across the water. The contrast between the eroded shoreline and surrounding coastal landscape makes it a memorable scenic stop near American River.
