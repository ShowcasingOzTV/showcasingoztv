Dunkeld (VIC)

Dunkeld is a picturesque village in western Victoria, positioned at the southern gateway to Gariwerd, the Grampians National Park. The town sits beneath the distinctive peaks of Mount Sturgeon and Mount Abrupt, where the rugged mountain landscape meets the open agricultural plains of the Southern Grampians.

The area forms part of the traditional country of the Djab Wurrung and Jardwadjali peoples, whose cultural connections with Gariwerd and the surrounding landscape extend across generations. Mountains, waterways, plants, wildlife and cultural places throughout the region remain important parts of this living connection.

European settlement developed during the nineteenth century as pastoral properties and small communities were established across the surrounding plains. Sheep grazing, wool production and farming became important to the local economy, while Dunkeld grew as a service centre for nearby stations and rural families.

The mountains immediately north of the town strongly influence Dunkeld’s identity. Walking tracks provide access to elevated viewpoints, rocky ridgelines, woodland and native wildlife habitat. The changing appearance of the ranges throughout the day creates a dramatic backdrop visible from streets, gardens and properties across the district.

Dunkeld has also developed a strong reputation for regional hospitality, food and wine. Local venues draw on produce from western Victoria, while accommodation businesses allow visitors to experience the landscape from the town centre, nearby farms and the foothills of the national park.

Community attractions preserve the town’s pastoral, military and social history. The local museum, heritage buildings and specialised collections provide insight into the people who lived and worked throughout the Southern Grampians district.

The Dunkeld Arboretum, town walking routes and surrounding lookouts offer quieter ways to experience the area. Together with the nearby national park trails, these places make Dunkeld a practical base for visitors interested in walking, photography, wildlife and regional heritage.

📍 Attractions

Dunkeld’s attractions explore local military history, community heritage, regional food and the pastoral properties that have helped shape the Southern Grampians district.

Explore attractions filming & feature options

  • Australian Light Horse Heritage Collection A specialised collection preserving equipment, uniforms, photographs, documents and stories connected with the Australian Light Horse. The displays provide insight into the service of mounted soldiers and the wider military history remembered by regional communities.
  • Dunkeld Bakery A local bakery contributing to Dunkeld’s country hospitality and town centre character. It provides visitors and residents with baked goods, refreshments and a convenient place to stop before exploring the surrounding walking trails and attractions.
  • Dunkeld Museum A community museum preserving photographs, documents, household objects, farming equipment and other material connected with Dunkeld and the surrounding district. Its collection traces local settlement, rural life and community development.
  • Mount Sturgeon Homestead A historic pastoral homestead situated near the base of Mount Sturgeon. The property reflects the district’s farming history and connection with the surrounding landscape, while continuing to operate as part of the Royal Mail Hotel’s accommodation and events offering.

🗺️ Points of Interest & Landmarks

Dunkeld’s landmarks combine community spaces, mountain walks and elevated viewpoints that reveal the dramatic meeting of the Grampians ranges and Victoria’s western agricultural plains.

Dunkeld Arboretum

The Dunkeld Arboretum is a landscaped reserve containing native and introduced tree species, walking areas, wetlands and open spaces. Views towards the nearby mountains make it a peaceful location for walking, picnicking, birdwatching and photography.

Dunkeld Town Centre & Heritage Trail Image Coming Soon

Dunkeld Town Centre & Heritage Trail

Dunkeld’s compact town centre contains historic buildings, local businesses and community landmarks. The heritage trail connects significant sites and interpretive information, allowing visitors to explore the town’s development on foot.

Grampians Valley / Mirranatwa Gap Lookout Image Coming Soon

Grampians Valley / Mirranatwa Gap Lookout

This scenic viewing area offers expansive perspectives across the Mirranatwa district, surrounding farmland and the mountain ranges of the southern Grampians. The changing light and broad landscape make it a striking regional viewpoint.

Mount Sturgeon Lookout Image Coming Soon

Mount Sturgeon Lookout

The Mount Sturgeon area provides elevated views over Dunkeld, nearby pastoral country and the southern reaches of Gariwerd. The landscape demonstrates the dramatic transition between mountain bushland and the open plains surrounding the town.

Piccaninny Walk Image Coming Soon

Piccaninny Walk

The Piccaninny Walk is a popular track in the southern Grampians offering a comparatively short climb through native vegetation to an elevated viewpoint. The summit provides broad views towards Dunkeld, Mount Sturgeon and the surrounding plains.
