Penneshaw (SA)

Penneshaw is a coastal township on the Dudley Peninsula at the eastern end of Kangaroo Island. Overlooking Backstairs Passage, it is the island’s main passenger and vehicle ferry gateway and the first Kangaroo Island community encountered by many visitors arriving from the mainland.

The town developed around Hog Bay, where its sheltered foreshore, jetty and maritime connections remain central to local life. Frenchman’s Rock recalls the visit of Nicolas Baudin’s expedition in 1803, while the Penneshaw Maritime and Folk Museum preserves stories from the district’s seafaring, agricultural and community history.

Penneshaw is also a practical base for exploring the wider Dudley Peninsula. The surrounding area brings together beaches, conservation parks, coastal walking trails, lighthouses, wineries, local produce and wildlife experiences, from Baudin Beach and Island Beach to Cape Willoughby and Antechamber Bay.

With essential visitor services, accommodation, dining and easy access to both natural and cultural attractions, Penneshaw offers more than an arrival point. It is a destination in its own right and a strong starting point for discovering the eastern end of Kangaroo Island.

📍 Attractions

Penneshaw and the Dudley Peninsula offer maritime adventures, wildlife encounters, local produce, wine, art and heritage experiences.

Explore attractions filming & feature options

  • Cape Willoughby Lighthouse & Weather Station Explore Kangaroo Island’s easternmost headland and the historic lighthouse precinct overlooking Backstairs Passage. The site reveals the maritime importance of this exposed coastline and the role of lighthouse keepers and weather observation in guiding vessels through the strait.
  • False Cape Wines A Dudley Peninsula cellar-door experience showcasing Kangaroo Island wines in a rural setting. Visitors can discover locally grown varieties and enjoy the landscape that helps shape the region’s distinctive cool maritime character.
  • Kangaroo Island Artworks Gallery A community-focused gallery at Baudin Beach presenting work by Kangaroo Island artists and makers. Its displays provide an introduction to the island’s creative community through locally produced art, craft and gifts.
  • Kangaroo Island Living Honey (Private Beekeeping Experience) A private beekeeping experience introducing visitors to Kangaroo Island’s protected Ligurian bee population, responsible hive management and the production of local honey.
  • Kangaroo Island Ocean Safari Boat-based coastal tours departing from Penneshaw, offering the opportunity to experience the island’s marine environment, rugged shoreline and wildlife from the water.
  • Penneshaw Penguin Centre A local wildlife experience associated with Penneshaw’s little penguin colony. Guided evening activities help visitors learn about the birds, their habitat and the importance of viewing them responsibly.
  • Rob’s Shearing & Sheepdogs A working-farm presentation featuring sheep shearing and trained sheepdogs, giving visitors an engaging look at the skills and traditions behind Kangaroo Island’s rural industries.

From historic coastal sites and sheltered beaches to remote lighthouses and conservation areas, these places reveal the landscape and stories of Penneshaw and the Dudley Peninsula.

Antechamber Bay & Lashmar Conservation Park

A broad sandy bay and protected coastal landscape beside the Chapman River. The area is valued for beach access, picnicking, walking and its peaceful bushland setting, with the Chapman River Bridge forming part of the approach.

Baudin Beach

A small seaside settlement west of Penneshaw with a sheltered beach, coastal views and a relaxed residential character overlooking Nepean Bay.

Cape Hart Conservation Park (Lashmar Road Terminus)

A remote conservation area on the Dudley Peninsula’s southern coast, protecting rugged mallee country and an exposed shoreline. The Lashmar Road terminus provides a sense of the area’s isolation and wild coastal character.

Cape St Albans Lighthouse Image Coming Soon

Cape St Albans Lighthouse

A remote navigational light on the peninsula’s eastern coastline, positioned above the waters of Backstairs Passage and the rugged cliffs around Cape St Albans.

Dudley Wines

A landmark cellar door overlooking Backstairs Passage, combining Kangaroo Island wine with expansive coastal views across the Dudley Peninsula.

Island Beach Image Coming Soon

Island Beach

A long, sheltered stretch of white sand on Eastern Cove, known for calm, clear water and broad views across Nepean Bay.

Kangaroo Island Sculpture Trail Image Coming Soon

Kangaroo Island Sculpture Trail

A walking trail close to Penneshaw featuring sculptures set among regenerating native vegetation, bringing together public art, community creativity and the local environment.

Penneshaw

Kangaroo Island’s eastern gateway and ferry port, with shops, visitor services, dining, accommodation and coastal viewpoints arranged around the Hog Bay foreshore.

Penneshaw Beach (Hog Bay, including Frenchman’s Rock)

A sheltered town beach beside the ferry port. Frenchman’s Rock commemorates the 1803 visit of French explorer Nicolas Baudin and connects the foreshore with the island’s early recorded European history.

Penneshaw Maritime & Folk Museum Image Coming Soon

Penneshaw Maritime & Folk Museum

A local museum housed in the former school building, preserving objects, photographs and stories connected with the Dudley Peninsula’s maritime, agricultural and community heritage.

The Ironstone Hill Hike (including Harry Bates’ Cottage Ruins)

A coastal walking route through the Cape Willoughby area, passing the ruins associated with early settler Harry Bates and offering views across the exposed eastern end of Kangaroo Island.

Turkey Corner

A scenic coastal point on the Dudley Peninsula, offering another perspective on the island’s eastern shoreline and surrounding rural landscape.
