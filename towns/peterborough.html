Peterborough (SA)

Peterborough is a historic railway town in South Australia’s Mid North, positioned between the state’s agricultural districts and the more arid country leading towards the Flinders Ranges and Barrier Highway. Its broad streets, substantial civic buildings and surviving railway infrastructure reflect a period when the town was one of Australia’s busiest and most strategically important rail centres.

Originally known as Petersburg, the settlement expanded rapidly after the railway arrived during the late nineteenth century. Peterborough became an unusual meeting point for three different railway gauges, linking passenger and freight services travelling between Adelaide, Port Augusta, Broken Hill and communities throughout the Mid North. The railways brought workshops, employment and generations of railway families to the town.

That history remains central to Peterborough’s visitor appeal. Steamtown Heritage Rail Centre preserves locomotives, carriages, workshops and the restored roundhouse, while the Printing Works and Town Carriage Museum reveal further stories about local industry, communication and everyday life during the railway era.

Peterborough continues to serve surrounding farming and pastoral communities. The landscape around town combines grain-growing country, open plains, low ranges and historic settlements such as Yongala, Morchard, Orroroo and Yunta. These places help illustrate the close relationship between transport, agriculture and regional development across this part of South Australia.

Today, visitors can explore the town’s railway heritage, local museums, public landmarks, nature reserves and surrounding scenic drives. Peterborough also provides accommodation, food, fuel and other services for travellers continuing towards the Flinders Ranges, Broken Hill or South Australia’s outback.

📍 Attractions

Peterborough’s attractions bring together nationally significant railway heritage, local history, creative experiences and the rural character of the surrounding district.

Explore attractions filming & feature options

  • Peterborough Printing Works A preserved historic print shop containing original machinery, equipment and displays associated with Peterborough’s newspaper and commercial printing history.
  • Peterborough Town Carriage Museum A railway carriage museum in the town centre presenting displays about Peterborough’s railway workers, passengers and the important transport network that shaped the community.
  • Quentin Park Alpacas & Studio Gallery A working alpaca farm and creative visitor attraction offering animal encounters, locally made products, gardens, a gallery, farm shop and hands-on experiences close to Peterborough.
  • Steamtown Heritage Rail Centre Peterborough’s signature attraction, preserving the historic railway workshops, roundhouse, locomotives, carriages and stories of the people who worked at this nationally important railway junction.
  • The Burg A local Peterborough attraction celebrating the town’s identity and community character, adding another distinctive stop to a visit focused on the district’s heritage and stories.

Goyder’s Line Memorial

Magnetic Hill
