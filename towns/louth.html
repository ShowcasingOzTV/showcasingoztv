Louth (NSW)

Louth is a small outback community situated on the Darling River in western New South Wales. Surrounded by vast pastoral country, the settlement reflects the isolation, resilience and strong local connections that have shaped life along the river.

Its river setting, historic buildings and pastoral heritage give Louth a distinctive sense of place. The community is also widely associated with the annual Louth Races, which bring visitors together for one of the best-known social events in this part of Outback New South Wales.
