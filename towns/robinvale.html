Robinvale, Victoria

Robinvale is a regional town on the southern bank of the Murray River in northwest Victoria. Surrounded by productive horticultural land and Mallee countryside, the town serves as an important agricultural, commercial and community centre for the surrounding district.

The area is part of the traditional Country of the Latje Latje people, whose connection with the Murray River and surrounding landscape extends across countless generations. The river continues to shape the environment, identity and daily life of the Robinvale district.

Robinvale was established during the 1920s and named in memory of Lieutenant George Robin Cuttle, who was killed during aerial combat near Villers-Bretonneux in France in 1918. This history led to Robinvale and Villers-Bretonneux establishing a formal twin-town relationship.

The town expanded considerably after the Second World War through the Robinvale Soldier Settlement Scheme. Returned servicemen and their families established irrigation properties, particularly for growing grapes and citrus, helping transform the district into a significant horticultural region.

Robinvale later became home to people from many different cultural backgrounds. Successive generations of migrants contributed to local agriculture, business and community life, giving the town a notably multicultural identity.

Visitors can explore Robinvale’s rural and settlement history, visit the historic Robinswood Homestead and spend time around the town centre and Murray River. The town also provides a convenient Victorian base for discovering this section of the Murray without shifting the focus to accommodation across the river in Euston.

🎟️ Attractions In And Around Robinvale

Robinswood Homestead

Explore a locally significant interwar homestead built for the Cuttle family in 1926 on the former Bumbang Homestead site. The house retains original architectural details and displays connected with the Cuttle family, Robinvale’s development and the town’s relationship with Villers-Bretonneux in France.

Robinvale Rural Life Museum

Discover Robinvale’s agricultural and settlement history through vintage tractors, working engines, farming equipment, household objects, photographs and handmade tools. The collection also includes material relating to the returned servicemen who established local fruit-growing properties through the postwar soldier settlement scheme.

📍 Landmarks, Points Of Interest & Hidden Gems

Robinvale Town Centre

Explore Robinvale’s principal commercial area along tree-lined Perrin Street and the adjoining Caix Square. The town centre contains local businesses, community facilities and memorials reflecting Robinvale’s military history, multicultural community and connection with Villers-Bretonneux.
