Portland (VIC)

Portland is a historic coastal city on Victoria’s far south-west coast, overlooking the sheltered waters of Portland Bay. Recognised as the location of Victoria’s first permanent European settlement, the city combines maritime history, a working deep-water port, heritage architecture and access to some of the state’s most dramatic coastal scenery.

The Portland district forms part of the traditional Country of the Gunditjmara people, whose cultural connections with the coast, forests, wetlands and volcanic landscapes extend back thousands of years. These living connections remain fundamental to understanding the region.

European settlement began when Edward Henty arrived at Portland Bay in 1834. The sheltered harbour supported whaling, fishing, shipping and agricultural trade, while the surrounding district developed through pastoral activity. Portland’s position eventually supported major road, rail and port infrastructure.

The city retains an extensive collection of nineteenth-century buildings that reflect its early commercial and maritime importance. Portland’s town centre, foreshore and harbour precinct bring together civic buildings, former warehouses, hotels, museums and working waterfront facilities.

Portland remains an active commercial port and fishing centre. Large vessels, fishing boats and recreational craft share Portland Bay, while the foreshore provides walking areas, beaches, lookouts and opportunities to observe seals, dolphins and migrating whales.

Beyond the city, Cape Nelson and Cape Bridgewater reveal volcanic landscapes, high sea cliffs, windswept beaches and important wildlife habitat. Visitors can explore the lighthouse, blowholes, limestone formations commonly known as the Petrified Forest and one of mainland Australia’s largest fur seal colonies.

Portland also serves as a base for the Great South West Walk, which connects coastal, forest and river environments across the wider Discovery Coast. Its combination of history, industry, wildlife and natural scenery makes Portland one of Victoria’s most varied regional coastal destinations.

📍 Attractions

Portland’s attractions introduce visitors to the city’s maritime history, working waterfront, coastal wildlife and distinctive heritage.

Explore attractions filming & feature options

  • Cape Bridgewater Seal Tours Seasonal boat tours providing close views of Australian and New Zealand fur seals around the rocky coastline of Cape Bridgewater. Dolphins, seabirds and migrating whales may also be observed when conditions permit.
  • Portland Cable Trams A heritage tram service travelling through central Portland and along sections of the foreshore. The journey links several visitor attractions while introducing passengers to the city’s history and coastal environment.
  • Portland Maritime Discovery Centre A waterfront museum and visitor centre presenting Portland’s maritime, whaling, fishing, rescue and settlement history through vessels, artefacts and interpretive displays.

🗺️ Points of Interest & Landmarks

Portland and the surrounding Discovery Coast contain historic streetscapes, active maritime precincts and some of Victoria’s most striking volcanic and coastal landscapes.

Cape Bridgewater Bay Image Coming Soon

Cape Bridgewater Bay

A sweeping crescent-shaped bay formed within the remains of an ancient volcanic crater. Its broad beach, high surrounding cliffs and exposed ocean setting make it one of the region’s signature coastal landscapes.

Cape Bridgewater Blowholes Image Coming Soon

Cape Bridgewater Blowholes

A rugged volcanic shoreline where strong Southern Ocean swells force water and spray through openings in the basalt formations. The effect is most dramatic during rough sea conditions.

Cape Bridgewater Petrified Forest Image Coming Soon

Cape Bridgewater Petrified Forest

An unusual collection of hollow limestone formations once believed to be fossilised tree trunks. The structures are now understood to be solution pipes created as mineral-rich water moved through coastal sand and limestone.

Cape Bridgewater Seal Colony Image Coming Soon

Cape Bridgewater Seal Colony

A major breeding and resting colony for Australian and New Zealand fur seals. The colony can be approached through authorised boat tours or viewed from the clifftop walking trail above the coast.

Cape Nelson Lighthouse Image Coming Soon

Cape Nelson Lighthouse

A historic lighthouse standing above the rugged cliffs of Cape Nelson. The surrounding precinct provides expansive ocean views and access to sections of the Great South West Walk.

Portland Foreshore & Harbour Image Coming Soon

Portland Foreshore & Harbour

Portland’s waterfront combines beaches, walking areas, fishing facilities, recreational boating and views across the working harbour and deep-water commercial port.

Portland Town Centre Image Coming Soon

Portland Town Centre

A historic commercial and civic centre containing early stone buildings, former hotels, churches, shops and public institutions that reflect Portland’s position as Victoria’s oldest permanent European settlement.
