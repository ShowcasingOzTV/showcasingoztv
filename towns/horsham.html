Horsham (VIC)

Horsham is the principal regional city of Victoria’s Wimmera, positioned beside the Wimmera River and surrounded by the broad agricultural plains of western Victoria. Its central location between Melbourne and Adelaide has helped establish Horsham as an important commercial, transport and service centre for communities throughout the surrounding region.

The district’s economy has long been connected with agriculture, particularly grain, pulse, oilseed and livestock production. Horsham supports these industries through agricultural research, machinery, transport, professional services and regional businesses, while also providing the major shopping, health, education and community facilities used by residents from across the Wimmera.

The Wimmera River is one of the city’s defining natural features. It curves through Horsham beside parkland, walking routes, picnic areas and recreational facilities, creating an accessible green corridor close to the city centre. The river also carries deep cultural significance for the Traditional Owners of the Wimmera landscape.

Horsham’s cultural life can be explored through its regional art gallery, public artworks, museums, performance venues and community events. The city centre combines longstanding local businesses with cafés, retail services and heritage buildings that reflect Horsham’s development as a major regional centre.

West of the city, Natimuk and Mount Arapiles introduce visitors to a dramatically different landscape. Mount Arapiles rises prominently from the surrounding plains and is internationally recognised for rock climbing, while nearby lakes, reserves and rural communities reveal more of the natural and agricultural character of the Wimmera.

Horsham is also well positioned for travellers exploring the wider region. The Grampians, or Gariwerd, lie to the south, while the highways extending from the city connect visitors with the Wimmera Mallee, the South Australian border and numerous smaller western Victorian communities.

📍 Attractions

Horsham’s visitor attractions explore the art, military history and rural traditions of the Wimmera. These experiences provide an introduction to the people, industries and stories that have shaped the city and its surrounding communities.

Explore attractions filming & feature options

  • Horsham Military Collection A locally assembled collection preserving military memorabilia, uniforms, photographs, equipment and personal stories connected with Australian service history and people from the Wimmera district.
  • Horsham Regional Art Gallery A major regional gallery presenting Australian photography, visual art, changing exhibitions and works connected with the culture and creative identity of Horsham and western Victoria.
  • Rosehaven Farm A rural visitor experience near Horsham offering an insight into local farming life, the Wimmera landscape and the agricultural traditions that continue to influence the region.

Horsham and its surrounding district contain river landscapes, historic transport infrastructure, rural townships, wetlands and dramatic rock formations. Together, these places help explain the history, environment and identity of the central Wimmera.
