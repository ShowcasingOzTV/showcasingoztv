Swan Hill (VIC)

Swan Hill is a vibrant regional city in northwest Victoria, positioned on the southern bank of the Murray River. Its riverfront setting, agricultural industries and role as a major regional service centre have made it one of the most important communities along this section of the Murray.

The Swan Hill district is part of the traditional Country of the Wamba Wamba and Wadi Wadi peoples, whose connections with the Murray River, nearby lakes and surrounding landscapes extend across countless generations. The river remains central to the area’s environment, culture and community identity.

European settlement developed around a river crossing and trading point during the nineteenth century. Paddle steamers transported passengers, wool, produce and supplies along the Murray, helping Swan Hill become an important centre for river trade, agriculture and regional commerce.

Swan Hill is particularly well known for the Pioneer Settlement, a large-scale living-history attraction recreating aspects of nineteenth and early twentieth-century life. Its buildings, working displays, vehicles and river experiences help visitors understand the importance of paddle steamers, farming and regional enterprise.

Agriculture continues to shape the district. Irrigated horticulture, grain production and livestock industries support the regional economy, while the Murray River and nearby lakes provide water, habitat, recreation and a defining sense of place.

South of Swan Hill, Lake Boga has its own distinctive wartime and aviation history. During the Second World War, the lake became the site of a major flying-boat repair depot. The Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum now preserves this nationally significant story.

Today, Swan Hill provides extensive retail, dining, accommodation, cultural and community services. Its combination of Murray River scenery, museums, agricultural experiences and nearby rural communities makes it a practical base for exploring northwest Victoria.

📍 Attractions

Swan Hill and Lake Boga offer visitors living-history experiences, galleries, agricultural businesses, gardens, wildlife and recreational attractions connected with the Murray River region.

Explore attractions filming & feature options

  • Hilltop Resort Fauna Park A visitor experience associated with Hilltop Resort where guests can encounter a selection of animals within a landscaped regional property.
  • Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum A museum preserving the history of the secret flying-boat repair depot established at Lake Boga during the Second World War. Displays include photographs, artefacts, underground communications facilities and a restored Catalina flying boat.
  • Murray Downs Golf & Country Club A major regional golf and hospitality destination across the Murray River from Swan Hill, offering an established course, dining, entertainment and visitor facilities.
  • Pheasant Farm A local agricultural attraction connected with the breeding and care of pheasants and other birdlife within the Swan Hill district.
  • Pioneer Settlement Swan Hill’s signature living-history attraction, recreating an early Murray River community through heritage buildings, working displays, costumed interpreters, historic vehicles and river experiences.
  • Raymer’s Lakeside Nursery and Gardens A nursery and garden destination near Lake Boga featuring established plantings, landscaped areas and horticultural displays.
  • Swan Hill Regional Art Gallery A public gallery presenting exhibitions from its permanent collection alongside touring displays and work by regional and Australian artists.
  • Temba Orchards A local horticultural business reflecting the importance of irrigated fruit production to Swan Hill and the surrounding Murray River district.
  • Tyntynder Homestead A historic homestead complex preserving buildings, furnishings, machinery and stories associated with early pastoral settlement in the Swan Hill district.
