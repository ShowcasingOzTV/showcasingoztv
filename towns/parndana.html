Parndana (SA)

Parndana is a small rural township located near the centre of Kangaroo Island, approximately 40 kilometres west of Kingscote. Surrounded by farmland and native vegetation, the town provides important services for central and western Kangaroo Island while offering visitors a practical base from which to explore several parts of the island.

The township was established following the Second World War as part of the Kangaroo Island Soldier Settlement Scheme. Returned servicemen and their families were allocated land across the central and western parts of the island, where they cleared scrub, established farms and created new community facilities under demanding conditions.

Agriculture remains fundamental to Parndana’s identity. Sheep, cattle, cropping and other rural industries continue across the surrounding district, while the town provides shops, fuel, education, sporting facilities, accommodation and community services for local residents and travellers.

The Parndana Soldier Settler Museum preserves the experiences of the returned servicemen and families who built the post-war community. Its collection includes photographs, documents, machinery, household objects and personal stories that explain how the settlement scheme shaped modern Kangaroo Island.

Parndana’s central position also provides access to the island’s rugged north coast. Stokes Bay, King George Beach, Snelling Beach and Western River Cove combine sheltered swimming areas, rocky headlands, river mouths, dramatic roads and expansive views across Investigator Strait.

The district was significantly affected by the 2019–20 Kangaroo Island bushfires, which damaged farms, native vegetation, wildlife habitat and infrastructure. The recovery of the surrounding communities reflects the same cooperation and resilience that characterised Parndana’s original soldier-settlement period.

Other Attractions In And Around Parndana

  • Australian Red Gum Gallery

    Located west of Parndana along the Playford Highway, the Australian Red Gum Gallery showcases handcrafted woodturning and decorative pieces created from distinctive Australian red gum and other Kangaroo Island timbers.

  • Parndana Soldier Settler Museum and Soldiers Memorial Park

    The museum preserves photographs, machinery, documents, household items and personal stories associated with Kangaroo Island’s post-war Soldier Settlement Scheme. The adjoining memorial area recognises the returned servicemen and families whose work created the Parndana community and surrounding farms.

  • Will’s Rare Breeds Farm

    Will’s Rare Breeds Farm is a conservation-focused farm attraction on the north-coast side of the Parndana district. It provides visitors with opportunities to see and learn about uncommon breeds of cattle, sheep, pigs, goats and poultry.

Constitution Hill – North Coast Road Lookout

Constitution Hill provides a dramatic elevated view along the winding North Coast Road towards Snelling Beach, Middle River and the surrounding hills. The descent is one of the most recognisable scenic approaches on Kangaroo Island’s north coast.

Image Coming Soon

King George Beach

King George Beach is a secluded north-coast beach set within a rocky cove near the mouth of King George Creek. Its isolated setting, clear water and surrounding headlands make it a quiet destination for fishing, walking and coastal photography.

Image Coming Soon

Parndana Town Centre

Parndana Town Centre provides shops, fuel, community facilities, sporting areas and visitor services for the central and western parts of Kangaroo Island. Its layout and memorial features reflect the town’s post-war soldier-settlement origins.

Image Coming Soon

Snelling Beach

Snelling Beach is a broad sandy beach at the mouth of Middle River, framed by steep coastal hills and reached by the scenic descent from Constitution Hill. Its remote setting offers striking views and a strong sense of Kangaroo Island’s rugged north coast.

Image Coming Soon

Stokes Bay Beach and Tunnel

Stokes Bay Beach is reached through a narrow natural passage between granite boulders. The passage opens onto a sheltered sandy beach where a rock pool provides protection from the stronger ocean conditions beyond.

Image Coming Soon

Stokes Bay Bush Garden

Stokes Bay Bush Garden presents a diverse collection of native plants, including species found on Kangaroo Island and elsewhere across Australia. Its garden setting highlights banksias, grevilleas, orchids and other distinctive Australian flora.

Image Coming Soon

Western River Cove

Western River Cove is a remote north-facing beach set beneath steep hills at the mouth of Western River. Reached by a winding coastal road, the cove provides a picnic area, beach access and habitat used by Kangaroo Island’s glossy black-cockatoos.
