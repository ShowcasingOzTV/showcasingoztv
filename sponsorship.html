Sponsorship Opportunities

Support ShowcasingOz TV and help drive a national tourism platform designed to promote Australia’s towns, regions, businesses, and tourism opportunities. From grassroots community support through to national strategic partnerships, every sponsorship tier plays a role in helping showcase more of Australia.

Why sponsor ShowcasingOz TV?

ShowcasingOz TV is designed to champion regional Australia through authentic travel storytelling, destination promotion, and local business exposure. Whether you are a small-town supporter, tourism body, council, regional business, or major national partner, your sponsorship helps us bring more visibility to the places and people that make Australia special.

Support Your Town — Be Part of the Journey

ShowcasingOz TV is not just being built for sponsors, investors, or major brands, it is also being built for the everyday Australians, regional supporters, small businesses, tourism advocates, and proud communities who genuinely want to help strengthen the visibility of the towns, regions, and destinations they care about.

These grassroots support tiers are designed for followers, locals, supporters, and community-minded businesses who may not be seeking large-scale commercial sponsorship, but still want to play a real role in helping bring more exposure to regional Australia, strengthen destination awareness, and actively contribute to the ShowcasingOz TV mission.

Every contribution helps support broader production, stronger reach, and greater visibility for the towns and communities we aim to showcase, because meaningful support does not only come from major partners.
