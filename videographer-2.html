About the Role

This is not your average videography role.

ShowcasingOz TV is seeking a highly capable Videographer / Editor / Co-Presenter for a rare opportunity to travel Australia as part of an ambitious national tourism media production — professionally capturing attractions, landmarks, accommodations, small businesses, regional communities, and extraordinary destinations across multiple states and territories.

This hybrid role is ideal for someone who wants more than repetitive local shoots.

It is designed for a creative who can confidently film, edit, and also step in front of the camera when required, building not only an exceptional and highly diverse portfolio, but potentially expanding their future opportunities through both production and on-screen experience.

This is a chance to help professionally showcase Australia while building a body of work and industry experience that could genuinely elevate your long-term creative career.

🎬 Why This Opportunity Stands Out

  • Travel extensively across Australia while filming an extraordinary range of destinations, businesses, and tourism experiences
  • Build a standout portfolio spanning tourism, travel, accommodation, regional storytelling, lifestyle, and commercial business content
  • Gain rare hybrid experience across filming, editing, and occasional presenting
  • Be involved in a national-scale production from the ground floor
  • Potentially strengthen both behind-the-camera and on-camera career pathways
  • Ideal for adventurous creatives who want to combine travel, storytelling, and serious portfolio development

What You'll Be Filming

  • Motels, hotels, caravan parks, and unique accommodation stays
  • Local attractions, tours, and tourism operators
  • Scenic landscapes, towns, and regional communities
  • Interviews with owners, operators, and locals
  • Small businesses such as restaurants, cafés, bakeries, mechanics, and other local operators
  • Behind-the-scenes footage and social media content
  • Occasional presenter support segments, walk-throughs, and on-camera contribution where required

At times, filming coverage may be split across different businesses or locations, meaning you may occasionally be required to film independently while the lead videographer captures content elsewhere. This role therefore suits someone confident enough to manage both production quality and professional communication on their own.
